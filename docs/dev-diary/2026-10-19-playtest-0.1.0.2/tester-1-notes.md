## Final report

Tester 1, the "common gamer" persona. All four games played through the text tool (play.exe), not the window. I also read mouseovers.md afterwards to see what a window player would have been told.

### 1. Verdict per Faction
- **Custodians (seed 501, Europe): no win by T30, Victory 51%.** The first 10 turns were the most fun of the whole session: countries flipping, the Moon race. The win itself never looked possible. Emissions stayed at 50-56 and the Sink at 10-19, a gap of 35 ppm, and nothing on the board told me which lever closes it. Taking countries made my own emissions worse.
- **Prospectors (seed 502, North America): WIN on T26.** Too easy. I owned all 20 Regions by T23 by selling Materials and buying Influence. The only real obstacle was finding the hidden Tech chain to my own gate (The Extraction Charter).
- **Arkwrights (seed 503, Europe): WIN on T30, one turn ahead of the Archivists (93%).** The best game. It was really about space: four Colony Ships, launch windows, the Moon, Venus, Mars and Mercury, then the Capital upgrade chain. Poor and slow early, but the race at the end was exciting.
- **Archivists (seed 504, North America): WIN on T21.** Easiest of the four: one Moon colony, a Research Directive, two ferry ships, then waiting for The Upload to appear on the shortlist. The last step, `upload 27 18`, was a single free order that took Victory from 40% to 100%.

### 2. Lost or bored vs excited
**Lost or bored**
- G1 T1: no price list and no list of what can be built. I guessed facility names until something was accepted.
- G1 T11: "Natural Sink grows by 1.5 ppm a turn" reads as +1.5 every turn. It is a single +1.5. After that I could see no path to my own win.
- G1 T17 and G4 T1-18: shortlists full of mining and transit Techs while my own gate never appeared.
- G2 T5: Venture Fund at 28/3,500 growing 6.5 a turn. No idea how it was supposed to be reached.
- G2 T23: I owned the planet and still couldn't win, because The Extraction Charter's chain was invisible. I had to read the glossary.
- G1 T28-30: whack-a-mole, buying the Arkwrights' new colonies every turn while they founded two more.
- G4 T9-10: a ship waited in orbit for two turns because the Habitat was queued behind the Archive.

**Excited**
- G1 T3: the first country flip ("The United Kingdom now belongs to Custodians").
- G1 T8, G2 T9, G3 T7, G4 T7: winning the race to the Moon. It was fun every time, although I won it 4 times out of 4.
- G1 T20: stealing China from the Prospectors in a bidding war.
- G2 T16: winning a battle "at 10%".
- G3 T18-19: landing on Mars and Mercury in the same turn.
- G3 T30: winning one turn before the Archivists.

### 3. Frustrations
- **Costs show up only when an order is refused.** G1 T4: I sold Fuel to buy Materials for a Colony Ship, and was then refused for Fuel, which nobody had told me the ship needed.
- **"ok" that wasn't.** G1 T9: refuel + load + transit all said "ok", but the ship arrived empty and no line said why.
- **The ship order rule is inconsistent.**
  - G3 T10 and G4 T6: loading from my own station used up the ship's one order.
  - Loading from a Region did not.
  - Refuel + load counted as one order.
  - None of this is explained anywhere.
- **Breaking an Accord silently.** G2 T19: `influence southafrica 45` was accepted and broke my Accord with the Arkwrights. Only afterwards did the report say "you broke it".
- **Invasions are buried.**
  - G2 T15: Kazakhstan "occupied by the Custodians" was visible only in the Nation States list.
  - G3 T15: I found out about Japan only when an order was refused. The headline that turn was about Nigeria.
- **No fighting back across oceans.** "not a neighbouring continent", and I never found a way to ship an Army.
- **Moving targets.** "needed" rises after you pay (G2 Japan: 32 then 35; Russia: 64 then 79), and Standing decays without warning until "could fall next turn" (G3 T27, home Region down to 14).
- **The 3-turn wait** before Leapfrog in a new Region isn't shown anywhere.
- **Victory % lies in both directions.**
  - It reads 100% while still gated (G3 T19-29).
  - It reads 22% while my emissions were five times over (G1).
  - It drops when colonists board a ship (G3 T11: 43% to 30%).
- **The top line flip-flops** between "Last turn to act: 47" and "On this path Collapse is not reached" from one turn to the next.
- **Accord offers stop every turn.** All three AIs offer non-aggression roughly every 4 turns, and the turn cannot end until I answer each one.
- **Techs finish that I never picked** (G3 T27-29, G4 T19). The report says "The Upload / Planetary Stewardship / The Extraction Charter is complete; Arkwrights led", but my pick was something else. In G3 this opened all three rivals' victory gates under my name. It might be two Techs completing in one turn, with the second picked for me.
- **Tool-only issues:**
  - The order list runs top to bottom, so `sell` must come before the purchase it pays for.
  - `influence tiangong` is refused because the error lists only Regions; you must use the colony number.
  - Battle reports are followed by about 20 copies of "calm enough: the Standing Army replenishes...".
  - Sea-level turns print about 20 "The sea took 1 coastal slot..." lines.

### 4. Exploits found
1. **Materials or Fuel to Ducats to Influence is a money printer.** By turn 15-20 Materials pile up at 85-170 a turn with nothing to spend them on. I sold 150-400 at once for 1-2 Ducats each and bought Influence at 4 Ducats a point. With that I took China + Europe in one turn (G2 T17), all 20 Regions by G2 T23, and China, India and Russia in G1. The AI defends with 5-25 a turn.
2. **Buying colonies off Earth with Influence from the armchair.** Tiangong (210), Stickney on Phobos, Swift on Deimos, and colonies on Mercury, Vesta and the Moon (90 each) all flipped in one turn, with no ship and no travel (G1 T27-29). This undercuts the whole colonisation race. It also wiped out the Prospectors and nearly stalled the Arkwrights.
3. **The Moon race is a free win for the human:** 4 out of 4, by turn 7-9, every time.
4. **Upload in one order:** stand 18 or more Colonists at the Archive and `upload <colony> 18` ends the game the same turn (G4 T21).
5. **Venture Fund grows far faster than its "banked" figure** (G2: about 100 shown as banked against 300-400 actually added per turn). I suspect objective payouts or something similar. Not an exploit as such, but it made the Prospectors' win much easier than the board suggested.

### Suggestions

#### Make it more interesting and compelling
- Keep and grow the race moments ("first to the Moon", "first to settle Mars"). Make the AIs actually contest them. Have the headline show a rival's progress ("Arkwrights have a ship 1 turn from the Moon") so it's a real race.
- Give every Faction one mid-game "big decision" card per phase (around T10, T20) that changes the path, for example "Seize China's grid: +X Ducats, +Y emissions". Right now turns 20-30 are repetitive: sell, buy Influence, flip, repeat.
- The Custodians need a visible plan. Show a "Stabilization meter": your emissions, the world's, the Sink, and the three biggest levers with their size in ppm ("Leapfrog China: -0.4 ppm", "Scrubber: -X"). I never knew which lever was big.

#### Balance
- **Cap or tax the Influence exploits:**
  - Raise the price of `buy-influence` each time it's used in a turn (4, then 5, 6...).
  - Or make the market price of Materials and Fuel fall much harder when large amounts are sold.
- **Make Influence on colonies off Earth need presence:** a ship of yours in that orbit, or a Relay or Embassy nearby. Or multiply the threshold by distance (Moon x2, Mars x4). Otherwise colonies are just Regions with a longer name.
- **Custodians:** emissions sat around 50-60 against a Sink of 8-19 in every game. A win needs about 40 ppm of cuts that no lever I found came close to. Either make Planetary Stewardship actually stack per turn (as its text reads), or make Leapfrog and the Scrubber cut several ppm, not tenths.
- **Archivists:** let uploads happen at most N a turn (say 4), or tie them to the Archive's size. That way the last stretch is a phase the rivals can respond to, not one order.
- **Moon race:** the AIs should build a Shipyard and ship by about turn 4. The report kept saying "Custodians had no Shipyard; Prospectors had no Shipyard".

#### UI fixes, adjustments and TEXT REDUCTIONS
- **Show costs before ordering:**
  - Each place's card (and `show` in the tool) should list what can be built there with its full cost, e.g. "Colony Ship 33 M + 35 F + 4 W". Today the cost arrives as a refusal.
  - Also flag what can't stand there ("Solar Array: stations only").
- **Warn before an order breaks an Accord.** Refuse it like other illegal orders, or require `--force`.
- **Invasions of your own Regions must be the HEADLINE:** "Custodians invaded Japan (your Army fled)".
- **Ships:** show "orders left this turn" per ship, and state the one-order rule in one place. Count colonists aboard a ship toward "off Earth", or label them "in transit".
- **Victory %:** when a gate or a missing piece holds the win, print "100% (held: needs X)" in the headline rather than a bare 100%.
- **Fix the flip-flop** between "Last turn to act: N" and "On this path Collapse is not reached". Show one stable figure with a trend arrow.
- **Text to cut:**
  - "Planetary Stewardship: ... the Natural Sink grows by 1.5 ppm a turn for the world" becomes "Natural Sink +1.5 ppm (once)". As written it promises a per-turn stack.
  - The repeated "calm enough: the Standing Army replenishes and Facilities run in full" (about 20 lines after every battle report): print nothing when a Region is calm.
  - The 20 "The sea took 1 coastal slot from X at +1.8 C. The coast moved one slot inland." lines become one line: "Sea rose at +1.8 C: you lost Power Plant (EU), Factory + Mine (Japan)...; rivals lost N Facilities."
  - "Japan passed to Arkwrights and threw them off at once: neutral." becomes "Japan revolted: now neutral."
  - Window hovers from mouseovers.md that run long:
    - The Unrest hover (5 clauses) becomes "Unrest 0-10: 3 stops Army refill, 6 halves Facilities, 10 revolt. Falls 0.5/turn."
    - The mothballed-Habitat hover ("A Habitat mothballed, or dark for want of Energy, still houses its Colonists. While more live here than...") becomes "Dark Habitats still house people; overcrowded colonies run at half."
    - The Allotment hover lists eight sources. Cut it to "Influence a turn: {n}. Unspent is lost."
  - mouseovers.md contains lines that look like notes rather than hovers ("I would like a window popup showing the modules of the space stations and", "for factions that have victory funds I'd like to see a condensed version of", "Population 12.2 (hundreds of millions) should say // something like..."). If any of these reach the game, cut them.
- **Accord spam:** add "refuse all offers this turn" (or a standing "decline Accords" setting). Don't make the turn wait on them.
- **What the window explains that the tool doesn't:**
  - The window explains "Last turn to act" and splits emissions by industry / Facilities / people. The text tool showed neither, and the emissions split is exactly what I needed as the Custodians. Put it in `show`.
  - The Venture Fund hover says selling isn't income. The tool doesn't.

#### Make the AI smarter
- Defend against Influence takeovers proportionately. When a rival's Standing is within about 20 of taking your home Region or capital, spend everything there, buy Influence, and stop spending 5 a turn on far-off targets. The Prospectors lost every Region in G1 and G2 while trickling 5-20 a turn.
- Contest the Moon: build a Shipyard and Colony Ship in the first 4 turns.
- Keep the gang-up-on-the-leader behaviour from G3 T22-30 (smears, agitation, invasions of the leader). It was the best AI moment. Trigger it earlier (at about 60-70% for the leader), and against Influence runaways too, not only colonists.
- Don't offer the same Accord the turn after the human refused it. Back off for 5 turns.

#### Accessibility in the first few turns
- Turn 1 should print a 5-line "what to do now" for your Faction. For example, for the Arkwrights: "1. Build a Station (22 M). 2. Shipyard there. 3. Colony Ship (33 M + 35 F). 4. Recruit Pioneers each turn. 5. Influence: cheapest neighbour is UK (30)." mouseovers.md shows the window has "A note at the head of each of the first N turns"; the text tool showed nothing like it.
- Show the Tech chain to your own victory gate from turn 1 ("The Upload <- Superconductors <- Orbital Data Centers"). I had to read the glossary in G2 and still couldn't find the chain in G4.
- Shrink `show` (tool): put the 20-region list and the giant flight table behind `show regions` / `show flights`. Show your own places first.

#### Bolster the colonisation side
- Add a ferry or "standing route" order (Axiom to the Moon, repeat). The 4-turn manual cycle per ship gets fiddly fast.
- Make where you settle matter. The yields (M 1.40, G 1.34...) never affected a single decision of mine. Add one unique resource per Body (Helium-3 on the Moon, ice on Ceres) that unlocks something.
- Stranded ships (G3 Mercury, 0.6 Fuel) should be able to scrap into the colony below for Materials or a free Module, so a one-way trip is a choice, not a waste.
- Let colonies make their own Pioneers or grow faster. Everything depended on Earth's 2-3 Pioneers a turn.

#### Improve conflict/war mechanics
- Let Armies ride Colony Ships or a troop ship across oceans and to colonies. "not a neighbouring continent" left me unable to defend Japan from Europe.
- Explain the odds. "Attacked at 10% to hold the field" and then winning felt random. Show "your strength 3 vs 5, 10% chance" before the fight (the window's hover seems to do this; the tool should too).
- War should be the counter to the Influence steamroll. When the Influence leader takes a Region, give the former owner a cheap "liberation" option (Army + Standing bonus) so taking land by money can be answered by force.

#### ONE MORE DIMENSION: Feedback and readability of cause and effect (most needed)
Across all four games the biggest problem wasn't a missing feature. It was that I couldn't see why numbers moved:
- the Fund growing 4x its "banked" figure;
- the Sink going down after a Break;
- "needed" rising;
- Techs completing that I never picked;
- Victory % jumping 43 to 30 to 100.

A strategy game lives on "I did X, so Y happened". I'd add a short end-of-turn "What changed for YOU" box: 5 lines, each with its cause, e.g. "Fund +312 (banked 100, objectives +212)", "Sink -2.6 (Break: The Sink Weakens)", "Japan threshold 32 -> 35 (rival spent 5, your Standing decayed 2)". This would also expose the suspected bugs above, because the player could see what the rules did.

### 5. Rating
**6/10.** The first ten turns of flipping countries and racing for the Moon really grabbed me, and the Arkwrights' multi-planet finish was great. But two cheap exploits (selling Materials into Influence, and buying colonies on other worlds by Influence) flatten everything after turn 15, and too much of what matters is invisible until an order is refused.
