## Final report

(Casual 4X player, played all four through the text tool `play.exe`, not the window. Where a point is about the tool, I say so.)

### 1. Verdict per Faction
- **Custodians (seed 601, Europe): WON on turn 28.** Colonist half done by T14 with stations over Earth. The emissions half looked hopeless (55 ppm over the Sink) until I found Scrubbers by typing a junk facility name on T21. Then two Scrubbers per Region plus Nature Reserves took the Sink from 10 to 63 in five turns. Fun from T15 to T25, but too easy once you know the trick.
- **Prospectors (seed 602, North America): WON on turn 31** (end of my turn 30). Investment Banks everywhere and an 80% Venture share filled the Fund easily. The best game of the four, because the Custodians fought me for my own capital (T15-T22). The win then waited about 5 turns on The Extraction Charter, and nothing told me it needs Rail Gun Freight.
- **Arkwrights (seed 603, South America): no win by T30.** 68/42 Colonists and 4/4 Bodies (Moon, Mars, Phobos, Venus) with score 1.00 and "Victory 100%", but held by Generation Ships. This was the most "space game" of the four: launch windows, flying Moon to Mars, first-to-settle moments. It had the poorest economy (1-5 Ducats a turn), and an AI Prospector runaway cooked the planet.
- **Archivists (seed 604, Japan): no win by T30.** Archive 225/225 and 25 Colonists ready on the Moon, but The Upload only started researching on T31. This was the most passive faction: little Materials, little Influence, out-bid for every Region, and the finish depends on a shared Tech I can't steer.

### 2. Lost / bored vs excited
- LOST: G1 T1 (no build menu and no costs; I guessed order names). G1 T1 (Stabilization looked impossible: "33.6 ppm over the Sink"). G1 T3-T10 ("Last turn to act" jumped between "safe", 48, 47 and 50 with no explanation; the window has a hover for it and the tool doesn't). G1 T18 (China "Pacified" and un-pacified in one Report). G2 T25 and G3 T26 ("Your Victory: 100%" while the win was still held).
- BORED: G1 T13-T20 (a 5-line conveyor belt while the Victory bar sat at 50%). G3 T26-T30 and G4 T29-T30 (done, waiting on a Tech).
- EXCITED: every "race to the Moon" win (G1 T7, G3 T8, G4 T9). Taking China by Influence (G1 T15, G2 T9). Scrubbers flipping Stabilization (G1 T21-T25). The USA tug-of-war (G2 T15-T22). Founding on Mars, Phobos and Venus (G3 T17-T25). The Refuel Accord rescuing my stranded ship (G4 T26).

### 3. Frustrations
1. The text tool never says what the board can build or what it costs. I only learned that Scrubbers exist from a refusal message (G1 T21).
2. "Your Victory: 100%" shows while the Tech gate still holds the win (G2 T25, G3 T26).
3. `tech extraction` -> "that Tech is not available yet", with no prerequisite named. I had to look in the glossary (G2 T25, G3 T26).
4. Accepting an Accord and spending Influence on that Faction's land in the same order list breaks the Accord at once, silently. It happened to me 3 times (G3 T14, G4 T10, G4 T24).
5. Battle odds appear only after the battle: "attacked at 0% to hold the field" (G3 T22), "4%" (G2 T18). The window has an odds hover; the tool has none.
6. Colony ids and orbit-slot numbers are different numbers for the same station (Axiom is colony 21 and orbit 2). This stranded a ship (G4 T23-T25).
7. Refuelling is fiddly: a ship refused for 0.1 Fuel (G1 T9), then needed three turns of change-orbit, refuel and fly.
8. Hundreds of Energy pile up with nothing to spend them on (`sell` takes only materials|fuel), in all four games.
9. Every 5 turns all three rivals re-offer non-aggression, and they keep offering the same "20 Energy for 20 Fuel" trade. The turn refuses until I answer each one.
10. Loading Colonists onto a ship drops "off Earth" (G1 T5: 11% -> 0%).
11. Some messages mislead: `lift southamerica 0 24` -> "6 Pioneers are waiting there"; `transit 26 earth low` -> "already there" when the ship needed `change-orbit`.

### 4. Exploits / too good
- **Buying a capital with Influence.** 20 bought Influence (80 Ducats) plus one Allotment flipped China, the Prospectors' home, with 6 Investment Banks and both its Armies (G1 T15). The same worked on the Custodians' China (G2 T9). The AI never defends its capital with Influence.
- **Stations over Earth count as "off Earth".** Lifting 2 Pioneers a turn onto a 44-Material station did the Custodians' and Prospectors' colonist half by T12-T14 without leaving Earth orbit.
- **Scrubbers.** 33 Materials each, done next turn, about 3 ppm of Sink each; together with Nature Reserves they beat every other climate lever by 10x.
- **Influence beats armies.** Occupied places snap back to whoever holds the higher Standing in the same Resolution (G1 T18, G3 T22). So war only wins where nobody has Standing.
- **Distress Call** (+5 Relations for idling one ship a turn) is a free lunch.
- **The Venture Fund grew far faster than "banked last turn".** It went 1933 -> 2499 (+566) with "90 banked", and ended at 7314/3500. It might be a bug or an unexplained rule.

### SUGGESTIONS

**Make it more interesting and compelling**
- Give each Faction 2-3 "signature moves" that drive its win, and name them on turn 1. Custodians: "Build Scrubbers and Nature Reserves; Leapfrog your Regions". Prospectors: "Investment Banks + Venture share". Arkwrights: "Colony Ships to four Bodies". Archivists: "Labs + Directive; Archive on the Moon". I found two of these by accident.
- Make the win's Tech gate part of the game, not a lottery. Show the gate Tech's path in the Victory line, e.g. "gate: The Extraction Charter (needs Rail Gun Freight -> Automated Refining)". Or let a Faction always add its own gate Tech to the Research Lead shortlist once its prerequisites are done.
- Add a mid-game rival crisis. When an AI is at 75% the Report already says so (G4 T25). Follow that with a card or Accord option that lets the table gang up on the leader.

**Balance**
- Scrubbers: cap at 1 per Region, raise the Energy upkeep, or make each one weaker the more of them exist. Today 12 of them solve the climate in 4 turns.
- Make stations over Earth count half toward "Colonists off Earth", or require at least one Colony beyond Earth orbit. That way the colonisation half actually needs ships.
- Raise the Influence threshold on a Faction's home Region steeply (for example x2), and let the AI spend its Allotment to defend when a rival's Standing passes 75% of the threshold.
- Arkwrights and Archivists get 1-6 Ducats a turn against the Prospectors' 80-170. Give them a better Ducat or Materials base, or a cheap way to turn their spare Energy into Materials.

**Make the AI smarter**
- Defend the home Region: answer a rival's Standing climb with Influence. I took two AI capitals in one turn each.
- Don't spam the same rejected trade ("20 Energy for 20 Fuel") or re-offer an Accord I refused the turn before.
- AI Prospectors should weigh Collapse (they took the world to "collapses on turn 34" in G3). A rational Prospector loses too if the world collapses.
- When a human leads (G1 T25, G2 T20), the AIs should co-ordinate against them: smear campaigns, Agitate, refusing Accords. Only the Custodians in G2 ever did.

**Accessibility in the first few turns**
- A 5-line turn-1 card per Faction: your win in plain words, your first three orders (for example "build module <your station> shipyard", "influence <cheap neighbour> N", "emigrants <home> 2"), and the one building that matters most. The window has a "note at the head of each of the first {} turns"; the text tool shows nothing like it.
- `show` is about 240 lines. Add `show --me` (my Stockpile, places, ships, owed answers, Victory line) and put the STILL OWED items (Accords, Trades, the card, the Tech pick) at the top of `show`, not only after a refused turn.
- Auto-answer repeats: "refuse all trades this turn" or a standing "accept non-aggression from X".

**Bolster the colonisation side**
- Make each Body matter: unique yields or one unique building per Body (Mars Research, Mercury Energy, Vesta Materials). Today the Moon's yields are close to Earth orbit's, so stations over Earth win.
- Let a Colony ship found with all aboard, not 4 (G1 T7, G3 T8 left 5 on board), or show "room 4: 5 stay aboard" before unloading.
- Show the launch-window calendar in the Report on the turn before a window, and in the ship line ("Mars window in 2 turns: 5t/18F").
- Give the Colonial Capital a visible role. The Arkwrights' turn-1 goal names it, but the Victory bar hit 100% without one.

**Improve conflict / war mechanics**
- Make Occupation stick: while a rival's Army occupies a place, Influence on that place should pause. Today Standing snaps it back the same Resolution, so armies are pointless.
- Show adjacency. "Not a neighbouring continent" (G3 T17) was the first I heard of which Regions touch. A line per Region, "neighbours: ...", would do.
- Show odds before the order (see UI), and offer a "retreat if under 30%" option.
- Inherited armies should come over on Hold, not Attack (G1 T17: they attacked Kazakhstan without my order).

**ONE MORE DIMENSION: Economy sinks and the resource loop**
I think this is most needed because in all four games I sat on 200-450 Energy and often 150+ Fuel with nothing to spend them on. Meanwhile Materials were the only constraint, which made every turn the same "buy Materials, build one thing". Concretely:
- Let Energy be sold, or let a building turn Energy into Materials, or make Energy-hungry buildings worth it (Scrubbers using lots of Energy would also fix balance).
- Give Fuel a use beyond ships: a refinery-to-Ducats rate, or a Fuel cost on Scrubbers.
- Show a one-line "your bottleneck: Materials" hint. Having three resources matters only if all three run short at some point.

### 5. Rating
**6/10.** The bones are good, but the wins come from undocumented tricks: Scrubbers, buying capitals with Influence, Earth-orbit stations. Then they stall on a Tech lottery. The race to the Moon, the Mars/Phobos/Venus flights and the fight over the USA show how good it could be.
