## Final report

I played all four Factions through the text tool (`play.exe`), not the game window, and won all four, well before turn 30: Custodians T22, Prospectors T23, Arkwrights T25, Archivists T18. I never lost a Region I cared about, never fought a battle on purpose, and the AI never came close to a win of its own. That is the headline: right now the game is a race against the clock with no opponent in it.

### (1) Verdict per Faction
- **Custodians**: the most interesting puzzle on paper (close a 33 ppm gap that is mostly other people's), and it ends as Scrubber spam. Influence buys you neutral Regions, each Region adds 2-4 Scrubber slots, and every Scrubber is +3 Sink for 33 Materials. Event and permafrost Emissions don't count for Stabilization, so the climate can't hurt your run. The energy crunch on T19 was the only real decision. Won T22.
- **Prospectors**: the Investment Bank is broken. +11.5 Ducats a turn for 23 Materials, as many as you have slots, Strip Permit doubles them, and income went from 16 to 300 Ducats a turn by T12. The Fund race was never in doubt. The only real constraint was that my gate Tech was three rungs deep and my Labs made 6 Research a turn. Won T23 while the planet ran at +78 ppm net a turn.
- **Arkwrights**: the best of the four, because Fuel (40 per Colony Ship) and the four-Body rule make you plan routes, and the Moon is a real slingshot (Mars costs 7 Fuel from the Moon against 20 from Earth). It's also the only game where the AI punished me: four Prospector Armies on my capital the turn my Accords lapsed. Then Influence handed the capital straight back to me, and the Colonial Capital was four turns of filling Module slots. Won T25.
- **Archivists**: trivial. I led every Tech, The Upload was done on turn 8, the Research Directive at 100% filled the Archive by T15, and one Moon colony plus a shuttle did the rest. Won T18.

### (2) Lost/bored vs excited
- LOST: G2 T16, Energy went from +25 to -56 a turn and I couldn't find a Report line saying the sea had taken my Power Plants. G4 T4, my gate chain's next rung wasn't on the shortlist, and nothing marks which of the three is "yours". G1 T7, I researched Green Consensus and Clean Power and saw no visible change in Emissions, and I couldn't see a per-source breakdown in the tool.
- BORED: G2 T11 onward (the Fund just ticks up), G1 T15-18 (two Scrubbers a turn), G3 T21-24 (Module-slot filling for the Capital), and G2 T11 and G3 T13, where the off-world part of the win was already met with nothing left to do but wait on the other half.
- EXCITED: G1 T6-7 (India for 34 Influence, first to the Moon), G1 T19 (the energy crisis turn), G2 T12-13 ("The United States could fall to Custodians next turn", my first real threat), G3 T7 (finding the Moon slingshot in the flight table), G3 T14 (the four-Army strike on Europe), G4 T16 (the Custodians going after my emptied Archive colony).

### (3) Frustrations
- An order addressed by index with no name echo: `change facility northamerica 3 decommission` scrapped my Launch Site after the sea had shifted the indices (G2 T16). The order list said "ok".
- G1 T13, Influence on a Region another seat occupied during the same Resolution counted as breaking our Accord, with no warning when I gave the order.
- The Colony Ship's 40 Fuel cost only shows up as a refusal (G3 T5).
- `build module <station> refinery` is refused "not on a station" when it IS a station (it means ground only).
- `build module-ducats` costs Ducats, but the help says "twice its Materials".
- Strip Permit is once per Region per game, and I found out only from a refusal.
- A station "needs 4 Pioneers here" without naming where "here" is.
- Tool-specific: the Report header repeats last turn's EVENT question after it has been answered (G1 T9), and `show` doesn't print the terms of an Accord or Trade offered to you; you only see them in the refusal text when the turn won't end. The window has an Energy-shortfall hover ("Next Income is {} Energy short. These go dark, in this order:"). The text tool has no such warning, which is how G1 T19 and G2 T16 caught me.

### (4) Exploits and dominant strategies
1. **Influence land-grab (all Factions)**: neutral Regions cost 22-41 Influence against an Allotment of 16-30, the AI almost never contests the one you pick, and the turn-2 non-aggression Accords the AI offers make your holdings immune to its Influence. Loop: Allotment, then the cheapest neutral Region, then a bigger Allotment. I held 13 Regions by T20 in G1 without moving an Army.
2. **Investment Bank stacking (Prospectors)**: Bank in every free slot (+11.5 Ducats for 23 Materials, so payback in about 5 turns), Industry Levels at 15-25 Materials, buy Materials with the Ducats, more Banks. Strip Permit doubles the Banks. Venture share goes to 80% at T10 and you win.
3. **Market buys during "Cheap Ore Offer"**: when someone refuses that card, Materials sit at 0.9 Ducats for two turns for everyone, with no cap on how much you buy. I bought 220 Materials in one order (G2 T8).
4. **Influence beats conquest**: an Occupation that completes on a Region where the old owner has more Standing flips straight back to that owner (G3 T16: Europe and Mexico). War burns a Factory and achieves nothing.
5. **Research Lead denial**: the Lead picks from three, so a human just never picks a rival's gate rung. The Archivists sat on a full Archive in G3 because I never offered The Upload. The Custodians never got Planetary Stewardship in G2.
6. **Custodians ignore the climate's own shocks**: Event and permafrost Emissions don't count toward the run, so "run them hot +2 ppm" is a free refuse.
7. **Research Directive 100% (Archivists)**: with the Lead and LLM, 38-43 Research a turn fills the 225 Archive in 6 turns.

### Suggestions

**Make it more interesting and compelling**
- Give every win a mid-game crisis that forces a trade-off. For example, the Archive could draw 12 Energy per Colonist waiting to upload, or the Fund could lose a share to "market crashes" whenever world Temperature crosses a Break, so a Prospector win at +2.2C costs something.
- Make the off-world half of the Custodian and Prospector wins harder: count only Colonists beyond Earth orbit, or require two Bodies. Two Earth stations with Habitats meet it by T11-13 today.
- Let the Colonial Capital tiering be a project with choices (which Modules, which Body) rather than six identical "build six, upgrade" turns: drop the requirement that every slot is built.

**Balance**
- Investment Bank: cap at one per Region, or make each extra Bank in the same Region pay half the one before. Exclude Banks from Strip Permit.
- Custodians: give the Scrubber a per-Faction cap (say 8), or count the Event and permafrost Emissions toward Stabilization at half.
- Neutral thresholds: scale with the number of Regions you already hold (+5 per Region held). The Archivists in G4 and the Custodians in G1 snowballed off cheap neutrals.
- The Upload on turn 8 is far too early. Raise rung-4 gates or have them need two rung-3 Techs. Also, the Research Lead shouldn't be able to keep a rival's gate off the shortlist forever: force a rival's gate onto the shortlist after N draws.
- Cap market buys per turn (for example, 30 Materials), or make the price rise with each unit bought.

**UI fixes, adjustments and TEXT REDUCTIONS**
- Echo the target's name on every index order ("decommission Launch Site (3) in the US"), and refuse when the index names a different building than last turn's listing.
- Mark the shortlist entry that is your own chain's reserved rung (a "(your road)" tag).
- Fix "not on a station" to "a Refinery stands only on the ground".
- Fix the help line for `-ducats`: say "for Ducats, twice its Materials' worth".
- Name the Region in "needs 4 Pioneers here" (for example, "needs 4 Pioneers waiting in a Launch Site Region of yours").
- Put a Report line in YourWorks when the sea destroys YOUR building: "The sea took your Power Plant in the United States".
- Text cuts from the window hovers (mouseovers.md):
  - "A warship stack on Blockade in a station's orbit starves it. A ground Colony starves while one rival alone holds Orbital Control of low orbit. / Starved, every Module makes nothing and still pays upkeep; nobody dies, nothing is destroyed. Each turn is an offence against you." becomes "Blockaded: Modules make nothing, upkeep still due."
  - "They are read into the Archive and leave the living population here: {} live at this place now. It cannot be undone once the turn ends, and the uploaded can never be lost -- not to a raid, a crowding death or a handover." becomes "Uploaded for good: {} here now."
  - "Free, and it takes a turn's notice: it lapses at the start of the next turn and you may act then. Acting against a term while it still stands is another matter -- it costs 3 and ends the whole Accord at once." becomes "Lapses next turn. Breaking it sooner costs 3 Relations."
  - The Research Directive hover: cut "What you keep back never reaches the Tech, so it counts nothing toward the Research Lead -- and the Lead is the only seat that picks what the table researches next." down to "Kept-back Research doesn't count toward the Lead."
  - The Blame mouse-over is listed in mouseovers.md with a "[BLAME_HOVER]" prefix. If that tag reaches the screen, it's a bug. (I couldn't check this: I had no window.)
- Tool: print pending Accord and Trade terms in `show`, and drop the stale EVENT question from the Report header once it has been answered.

**Make the AI smarter**
- Contest the human's Influence target: if a human has Standing over half the threshold on a neutral Region, the AI should outbid. In four games the AI contested one Region of mine once (Arkwrights, North Africa, G1).
- Re-sign or arm before Accords lapse. The AI let lapses happen and exploited one only once (G3 T14). The Prospectors in G2 never attacked the most obvious target, my undefended Investment-Bank Regions.
- Use the Research Lead to deny the leader's gate (I did it every game; the AI picked my gates for me).
- When conquering, follow up with Influence on the conquered Region so it doesn't flip straight back.

**Accessibility in the first few turns**
- On turn 1, show each Faction's three biggest levers with costs: Custodians "Scrubber 33M = +3 Sink", Prospectors "Investment Bank 23M = +11.5D/turn", Arkwrights "Colony Ship 33M + 40 Fuel, Moon 1 turn", Archivists "Archive 55M at a 4-Colonist colony off Earth".
- Print the gate's full chain with Research totals in the Victory block ("The Extraction Charter: Automated Refining -> Rail Gun Freight -> Charter, 142 Research").
- A turn-1 table of build costs. Discovering costs only by issuing orders and reading the leftover stockpile is how I spent my first ten minutes.

**Bolster the colonisation side**
- Bodies should differ in more than yields: Mars dust storms that idle Generators, Venus stations that need Energy imports, Mercury's Fuel cost. Right now I settled the cheapest Body (the Moon) in four games out of four, and nothing else ever mattered except for the Arkwrights' Body count.
- Make the Moon contestable. I was first to the Moon in 4/4 games because no AI builds a Colony Ship before T6.
- Let a Colony's Body matter for the win: count Colonists by distance, or give points for the first colony past the asteroid belt.

**Improve conflict/war mechanics**
- Fix exploit 4: on a completed Occupation, raise the occupier's Standing to the old owner's plus the margin, or halve the old owner's Standing.
- Let Armies garrison and reinforce automatically from a Barracks. My UK Armies sat dug in for 10 turns doing nothing.
- Make space war reachable: no Ship of any seat ever attacked another in four games. Give an obvious target, for example a Colony Ship in transit that can be intercepted with a "Raid: steal Colonists" outcome.

**ONE MORE DIMENSION: interaction between the win conditions (a "table pressure" layer).** This is the most needed, because every game I played was four solitaire races. Nothing a rival does slows my bar except, rarely, Influence on my capital. Add one lever per Faction that directly attacks another's bar:
- Custodians can Sanction a high-Blame Faction's Banks or Factories (half output while sanctioned).
- Prospectors can Buy Out a Colony (Ducats for a rival's off-world colony at a price set by its Standing).
- Arkwrights can Poach Pioneers from a neighbouring Region.
- Archivists can Embargo Research (deny a rival's Research toward the Lead).
Then the AI, which already reads "who is leading", has tools to gang up on the leader.

### (5) Rating
**5/10.** The bones are excellent: the per-Faction victory puzzles, the flight table and launch windows, the climate panel and Influence as a real economy. But every Faction has a single loop that wins by turn 18-25 against an AI that never stops it, so a hardcore player has nothing to play against after turn 10.
