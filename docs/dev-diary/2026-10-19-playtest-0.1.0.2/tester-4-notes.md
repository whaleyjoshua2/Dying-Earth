## Final report

Tester 4, the colonisation dreamer. Four games through the text tool (`play.exe`), not the window. I did read `mouseovers.md` once the coordinator opened it, and I say below where the window tells players things the tool doesn't.

### 1. Verdict per Faction
- **Custodians (seed 801, Europe, played to T30):** First on the Moon (T7) and Mars (T24); four colonies, 57 Colonists off Earth. The colonist half of their win was done by T14, then Victory sat at **50% for 16 turns** because Stabilization depends on the whole world's emissions (71 against a Sink of 29). Colonising is pointless for this Faction's win after turn 14, so it's the wrong Faction for me.
- **Prospectors (seed 802, North America, ended T26, Archivists won):** The best pacing for the frontier: Moon T10, Mars T12, a Gateway station T20, six colonies. Their own goal (bank 3,500 Ducats) never engaged me. Victory read 59% while the Fund was at 17%.
- **Arkwrights (seed 803, South Africa, played to T30):** The colony Faction in feel: a 22-Materials station with 4 Colonists on turn 1, 9-seat ships, +1 Influence per Pioneer lifted. I reached Mars, the Moon, Venus (a cloud station) and Phobos. But they're poor (Energy at 3.5, Ducats under 20), and they **can't research their own gate** (Generation Ships) because their Labs make 2 Research a turn. That left Victory at "100%" and not winning for six turns.
- **Archivists (seed 804, Japan, I won at T27):** The strongest Faction. They lead Research, Observatories feed the Archive, and The Upload completes in one turn. Uploading Colonists is the most loaded story choice in the game, and the game treats it as a counter.

### 2. Lost or bored vs excited
- **Excited / captivated:** ship names ("TSV Magellan arrived at the Moon", G1 T5); every first landing ("win the race to the Moon ... Arkwrights had Colonists aboard; Custodians had no Shipyard", G2 T10); first on Mars (G1 T24, G2 T12, G3 T12, G4 T24); the Venus cloud station "Arkwrights built Ishtar over Venus" (G3 T21); Phobos (G3 T24); the Break texts ("Greenland and Thwaites pass the point of return"); the Distress Call and Surplus Habitats cards.
- **Lost / bored:** Victory frozen at 50% (G1 T14-30) and at 100%-but-held (G3 T25-30); picking Techs blind with no tree in the tool (G4 T7-24); spending big Influence allotments when no neutral Regions were left (G3 T25); Arkwrights and Archivists sitting on piles of Energy or Materials with nothing colonial to spend them on.

### 3. Frustrations
1. **The 15.6 / 15.7 Fuel trap.** It happened in G1 T9, G2 T16 and G4 T9. A full tank, less Earth to Moon (15.7) and Moon to Earth (3.7), leaves 15.6: a tenth short of a second trip. Every shuttle costs an extra turn to change orbit and refuel.
2. **Ferry logistics.** Each ship takes one order a turn, so one shipload is change-orbit, refuel, load, fly, unload: up to five turns. Pioneers recruited in Regions without a Launch Site (UK 14, Japan 8 in G1) can only be loaded from low orbit, which I learned from a refusal message.
3. **"Orbit contested" with an Accord standing.** A rival Frigate on Hold in low orbit stopped my landing at my own colony (G1 T12, G3 T15). In G3 we had non-aggression AND passage. 9 to 10 Colonists were stuck aboard.
4. **New colonies hold 4.** I unloaded 12 Colonists on Mars and 4 founded the colony; the rest sat in orbit. Both `unload` lines said "ok".
5. **Accords broken by accident**, twice (G1 T21, G4 T8). The tool accepts the order as "ok ... breaks your Accord with X" and ends the turn.
6. **Influence evaporates.** India was sniped at 35/40 (G1 T9), again at 33 (G3 T21), and China in G4 T9. There is no refund and no "you came second".
7. **Forced to research a rival's win gate.** In G4 T26 the "free choice" was Generation Ships alone, with the Arkwrights at 0.90.
8. **Repeat Accord offers.** Rivals re-offer non-aggression one turn after we struck non-aggression plus passage, every game, and each offer must be answered. Accords lapse every 5 turns, so re-offering them is a chore.
9. **Victory % misleads.** 59% with the Fund at 17% (G2). 100% shown while held by a Tech and a missing Capital (G3).
10. **Endings are a receipt.** The game ends on one headline line, and a win and a loss read the same.

### 4. Exploits / broken-feeling things
- **Earth-orbit stations count as "off Earth".** In G3 T24, `lift southafrica 30 24` put 30 Pioneers into my Orbital Reef in one order: +30 Influence from the Spaceport and +30 Colonists off Earth (64/42). No ship needed. In G1 the ISS alone met the Custodians' 18 by T9. Most "off-world" Colonists in my games were 400 km up.
- **The AI never goes for Mars early.** I was first on the Moon 4/4 and first on Mars 4/4. Taking the first Mars window (T7) makes Mars yours.
- **The Archivists are much stronger than the rest.** The AI Archivists won G2 at T26 and were at 1.00-held by T30 in G1; I won with them at T27. The only thing slowing them is how long the Research Directive takes to fill the fund.
- **Research Directive sinks the Research Lead.** What you hold back "counts nothing toward the Research Lead". The window's hover says so; the text tool doesn't. The Arkwrights' 50% Fuel directive locked them out of their own gate.
- Possible display bugs: Orbital Reef read "Modules 5/0" with 0 Colonists (G3 T23). The Custodians showed Blame 0.00 in G4 T22. A coast-loss headline said "+1.8 C" while the Climate line read +1.28 C (G2 T4, again G3 T3).

### SUGGESTIONS

**Make it more interesting and compelling**
- Name your colonies and ships (`name 27 Shackleton Base`). Ships already have lovely names; colonies are "colony 27". It's the cheapest joy in the game.
- A one-line flavour status per colony tier, in the Break-text voice ("Mare Imbrium: the first children born under Earthlight").
- A real ending: an epilogue screen listing each colony, its people and who founded it first, plus the Archive's "18 remembered".
- Make the Upload a story moment: a line naming the place and count ("Ten of Mare Imbrium's people were read into the Archive"), and the colony card keeps "10 remembered here".

**Balance**
- Count only Colonists beyond Earth orbit toward "off Earth", or count Earth-orbit stations at half. This closes the lift exploit and makes Moon and Mars matter.
- Custodians: give colonies a real lever on Stabilization, e.g. colonists off Earth lower per-person emissions, or a colony Scrubber module. Otherwise their colonising is wasted after T14.
- Arkwrights: give them a Research floor toward Generation Ships (e.g. each Body they hold adds Research), or make Generation Ships theirs to pick whoever leads. Raise their starting Ducats from 1, and fix the Energy deficit (a cheap Solar Array on Orbital Reef from the start).
- Archivists: slow The Upload, e.g. a cap of N uploads a turn, or the Archive must run with power for K turns first.
- Never offer the Research Lead a single Tech that is a rival's win gate. Always show at least two options.

**UI fixes, adjustments and TEXT REDUCTIONS**
- Refuse an order that breaks an Accord unless it carries an explicit word, e.g. `influence southasia 17 break`. Don't accept it as "ok".

**Make the AI smarter**
- Use the Mars window. Four games running, no AI took Mars early.
- Stop re-offering an Accord that already stands. Offer to renew in its last turn instead of letting it lapse.
- The AI Prospectors snipe Regions the human is visibly climbing. That's fine, but other AIs should push back (counter-Influence, agitate), not just the human.
- Have AIs contest a rival who is one step from winning (G2 Archivists at 16/18 uploaded: nobody did anything).

**Accessibility in the first few turns**
- Turn 1 should say plainly: "Build a Shipyard on your station, then a Colony Ship. Nation States cannot build ships." I only learned it from `help`.
- Show the next Mars window from turn 1, as a countdown in the top bar ("Mars window in 6 turns").
- Show each ship's round-trip Fuel need ("Moon and back: 19.4 of 35").
- Say at the start that a Colony with a Refinery refuels ships in its low orbit. I found out at G2 T23 from a refusal message.
- The text tool needs a Tech tree view (`show techs`) with prerequisites. The window has one; the tool doesn't. (tool)

**Bolster the colonisation side (my specialty)**
1. **Auto-refuel at home.** A ship in Earth's low orbit tops up from a Launch Site you hold, paying from the Stockpile. That kills the 15.6/15.7 trap and two turns of every trip.
2. **Standing ferry routes.** A `route <ship> <from> <to>` order that loads, flies, unloads and returns on its own until cancelled. This turns logistics into a decision instead of chores.
3. **Let load-and-launch happen in one turn** for a Colony Ship at its yard ("board and go").
4. **A colony ship becomes the colony.** On founding, the whole crew lands. Extra people go into "crowded" (half output) rather than staying in orbit, as the Habitat rules already allow.
5. **Refugee Convoy should offer "Send them to the stars":** the refugees become Pioneers at a Launch Site, or arrive straight at your largest colony. That card is the game's theme and it currently offers +1 population or -5 Standing.
6. **Colony growth that you can see.** Each tier unlocks something you notice: Settlement gets a name and flavour line; Colony unlocks a local Shipyard discount; Colonial Capital raises the Body's flag and gives a Moment. The Settlement-to-Colony step at 4 Widgets a turn on Mars is about 9 turns of waiting; let colonies make more Widgets as they grow.
7. **Distinct Bodies.** The Moon's four sites were all taken by T15 in each game. Then nothing was left nearby except stations. Give the Moon more sites or let Settlements spawn outposts; give Venus clouds, Mercury heat and Ceres ice one unique module each.
8. **Colony events, not just Region events.** Cards like Surplus Habitats and Deep Survey were the best: more about births, a dust storm on Mars, a first harvest, a colony asking for independence.
9. **Make colonies matter to every Faction's win**, not just as a counter that maxes out early.

**Improve conflict/war mechanics**
- Orbital Control should respect Passage: a partner's warship on Hold must not block your landing. If blocking is intended, say "Arkwrights Frigate holds the orbit; offer passage or bring a warship" at the order, not after the turn.
- Give colonies a defensive option the colonist player can afford (a Battery module is listed; tell the player when a rival warship arrives at their Body).
- Make war over colonies legible: a "threats" line in the colony card (rival warships here, rival Standing climbing).

**One more dimension: STORY / IDENTITY of the colonies**
This is what I think is most needed, because every high point I logged was a story beat: a ship's name, a first landing, a cloud city, a Break text. Every low point was a counter that wouldn't move. Colonies should have names you choose, a short history ("founded T7 by TSV Magellan with 4 settlers"), milestones (first 10 people, first child born, first Capital), and an ending that recites them. It costs little and it is the difference between "spreadsheet of Modules" and "my people on Mars".

### 5. Rating
**6 / 10.** The colonisation moments are genuinely thrilling: first on the Moon, first on Mars, a station over Venus. But too often fiddly ferry logistics, a Fuel gotcha and victory bars that don't move bury the frontier under spreadsheet admin.
