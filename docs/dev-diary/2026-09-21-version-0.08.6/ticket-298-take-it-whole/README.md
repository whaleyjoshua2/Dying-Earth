# A place taken whole: the destruction rolls narrowed to the Occupation that times out

Ticket [#298](https://github.com/whaleyjoshua2/Dying-Earth/issues/298) on
[map #289](https://github.com/whaleyjoshua2/Dying-Earth/issues/289).

No picture: the rule has no surface of its own beyond the Climate Panel's war hover, whose words
moved, and the Moment for a place taken by force, which now fires on a Pacified take with *taken
whole* in its text. The measurement, twenty seeds with the Custodians first:
[`sweep-20-custodians-first.txt`](sweep-20-custodians-first.txt).

## What was decided, in the designer's words

- *"q1 i"* -- **whole-place**: the three-turn clock rolls a quarter per building, Pacified rolls
  nothing, the roll on the attack itself goes.
- *"q2 yes"* -- the Scrubber and the Archive keep their own rules.
- *"q3 allow unique facilities to be treated like others for purposes of this roll"* -- a Unique
  rolls like any other; the glossary, which said it never burned, is corrected.
- *"q4 leave it"* -- war ppm per building stays at 2.
- *"q5 yes"* -- the Moment fires on every take by force, burned or not.
- *"q6 nothing new"* -- the computer is unchanged.

## Settled by the builder, to be corrected if wrong

- The rolls are called from one place now, the transfer, and only when the reason is not
  *Influence* and not *Pacified*; the Battle's call is gone. The Moment moved out of the rolls and
  onto the transfer, so it fires whether or not anything burned: *Europe taken whole by the
  Custodians (Pacified)* against *Europe was taken: Factory, Refinery destroyed*.
- The Climate Panel's war hover, the `climate.toml` and `influence.toml` comments, and the
  **Battle**, **Occupation**, **Pacified** and **Unique Facility** glossary entries say the rule as
  it is now.

## Measured

by force, so the rule was not exercised in this batch. The ground war on this seating stopped
under the defence ticket's rule (see ticket #296's diary); the closing sweep reads all four
seatings, where the Prospectors-first seating opened 49 ground Battles last version.
