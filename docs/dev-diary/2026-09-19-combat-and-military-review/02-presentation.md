# Combat as the player sees it — presentation review (0.08.4, read-only)

*Filed from the review session of 2026-09-19 (evening), where a general-purpose agent wrote it as a read-only report against `main` at version 0.08.4, with a brief that said: cite every rule as file:line, mark every figure from a sim log as measured behaviour, never run the game bare, never present a change as decided. Proposals only; nothing here is decided.*

Pictures (filed beside this report, every one taken headlessly in `shot:` mode): `mil-tall-earth.png` (China card with the Army orders block, 1920x2400 — the block is below the fold at 1080), `mil-region-earth.png` (same card at 1080: block not in frame), `mil-region-solar.png` (roster + Solar map stacks), `mil-region-mars.png` (orbit band), `mil-stack-mars.png` (Ship stack card with odds), `mil-report-report.png` and `mil-moment-report.png` (four-Faction Battle at Mars in the Report; `moment:battle` opened the Report, not a Moment — see below), `mil-threat-earth.png` (Faction window: Holdings and Under way).

## What the player sees today

**Army orders on a Region card** (`src/ui.rs:4779-4800`). Only if the player directs the Region and has a live Army there. A `Stance:` row of selectable labels Attack / Hold / Evade (Intercept is Ships-only, `src/ui.rs:3805-3807`), one row for the whole stack, then per Army a line "the 1st Chinese Army (strength 4, standing):" and one button per neighbour: "move to X" if the player controls X, else "attack X (61%)" (`src/ui.rs:4786-4792`). The percentage is `first_round_odds(mine, defenders)` (`engine/src/combat.rs:137-144`): p^3 + 3p^2(1-p), i.e. the chance of winning a best-of-three of first-round hit rolls — not the chance of winning the battle, and the label never says which. The button carries no defender name, no defender strength, no holder, no cost (a move is free, so `cost_button` draws it bare, `src/ui.rs:3722-3723`), and the hover is only what `cost_button_with_hover` composes, which for MoveArmy is nothing (`src/ui.rs:3778-3789`). "Repair fully" buttons appear only when damaged. The block sits at the very foot of a long card, after Pioneers, Unrest and the Launch Site line (`src/ui.rs:4661, 4759, 4779`): at 1080p it is off-screen without scrolling (`mil-region-earth.png` vs `mil-tall-earth.png`). The Armies list higher up reads "the 1st Chinese Army (Custodians, standing): strength 4, damage 0/5" (`src/ui.rs:4617`).

**Colony card**: Armies listed (`src/ui.rs:4976-4980`), a stance row and repair buttons (`src/ui.rs:5071-5079`); no attack target, since ground attack on a Colony is by landing from a Carrier: "Land the Army to attack Tycho" (`src/ui.rs:5313`) with no odds at all.

**Ship stack card** (`src/ui.rs:5133-5190`, `mil-stack-mars.png`): per-Ship "TSV Valiant: strength 3, damage 0/4", the stance row, then "Against Prospectors 0, Arkwrights 0 and Archivists 0 (0 in all). Attack odds (first round): 100%" and an "Attack this turn" button that opens a confirm line (`5181-5190`). A rival's stack card shows the same odds sentence from the player's side (`5147-5168`). The odds are again first-round only; the stack line for an unarmed rival still offers an attack.

**Queued order feedback**: the order joins "Orders this turn (n)" as "Armies at China: Attack" / "the 2nd Chinese Army to Russia" / "Ships at Mars: Attack" (`src/ui.rs:2985-2996`, `3375-3378`); the stance label highlights the pending stance (`3812-3816`). Nothing on the map changes.

**Roster** (`mil-region-solar.png`): Ships rows "TSV Valiant (frigate) at Mars - strength 3, 30/30[, damage 3/4][, an Army aboard]" (`src/ui.rs:3189-3210`), transits "in transit to Mars, 2 turn(s) left" (`3221-3230`); Armies rows "the 1st Chinese Army, at China: strength 4 (Hold)" with a six-line stance tooltip (`3259-3279`). Carrier cargo is the words "an Army aboard" on the roster and "carrying an Army" on the stack card (`3204`, `5139-5143`). **Under way** (Faction window, `src/ui.rs:6676-6695`, `mil-threat-earth.png`): "In transit: TSV Vanguard, Earth to Mars, 3 turns" — own Faction and rivals alike; Holdings counts "2 Ships, 1 Army" (`6654-6668`).

**Report**. The Battle Report is a block at the *bottom* of the dispatch, after "Your works" (`src/ui.rs:7119-7142`): place name, one line per party in its colour — "Custodians, attacking: Frigate, Frigate, strength 6, 3 hit(s) landed; destroyed: none; escaped: none" — then "3 round(s). Nobody holds Orbital Control." (`engine/src/resolution.rs:355-362, 380-384, 420`). Unit names in it are *types*: combatants are named "Custodians Frigate" / "Custodians Standing Army" (`engine/src/resolution.rs:68, 78`) and `run_melee` strips the first word (`389-393`), so the Ship names of 0.08.1 and the Army names of 0.08.4 never reach the Battle Report. A Battle is not a `ReportLine`, so it cannot headline: in `mil-report-report.png` a four-Faction melee at Mars sits under 20 "have not forgiven" lines while "Coastal Engineering is complete" is the headline. Battle-related cards (`assets/data/report.toml`): `army_moved` ("{faction} Army moved from X to Y and attacks", `LineKind::Army`, `resolution.rs:241-250`), `army_landed`, `ship_destroyed` (`LineKind::DecisiveBattle`, rank 4, `resolution.rs:449-469`), `units_destroyed`, `occupation_begun` (rank 5), `control_changed` (rank 2, with a Moment), `threw_off`, `landing_contested`, `unrest_threshold` (carries "the Standing Army no longer replenishes", `engine/src/state.rs:3308-3322`; the same note is the green/amber Unrest line on the card, `src/ui.rs:4524`). Rival deeds say "moved an Army into X", "set its Ships at Mars to Attack" — after the fact.

**Moments**. `MomentKind::DecisiveBattle`, rank 4, named "A Battle that cost a unit" (`engine/src/report.rs:207, 225`), card "A Battle at {place}: {result}" (`report.toml [moments.decisive_battle]`). It fires in exactly one place: `destruction_rolls` (`engine/src/resolution.rs:526-534`), i.e. when a place *taken by Occupation* loses Facilities. A Ship destroyed in battle produces a Report line but no Moment (`449-469`); a destroyed Army produces *nothing* — `destroy_army` (`471-478`) writes no line, no Moment; the loss appears only inside the Battle Report's "destroyed:" list as "Custodians Army". `moment:battle` with `battle:1` therefore opened the plain Report (`mil-moment-report.png`).

**Map**. Earth: one numbered shield per Faction with Armies at a Region, grey for neutral (`src/ui.rs:2453-2470`, shield at `764`); no stance, no damage, no "moved this turn". Solar map: one stack marker per seat at a Body (`src/scene.rs:258-271`), labels "Custodians x1 str 3" (`src/ui.rs:2383-2394`), and every Faction's transits drawn as a line with a marker and "TSV Valiant (frigate): 2 turn(s)" (`src/scene.rs:287-300`, `src/ui.rs:2395-2409`) — so an approaching fleet *is* visible, with its ETA, but not its destination in words or its strength. Orbit band per Body: "Prospectors: 1 Ship(s), strength 0" (`2480-2500`). Nothing draws a Battle: `report.battles` is read only by the Report (`src/ui.rs:7119`), and `BattleLine::aggressor` ("for the log and the map", `engine/src/state.rs:883`) has no caller.

## What they cannot see
- Who defends a Region they are about to attack, at what strength, with what damage, and what "61%" is a chance *of*.
- Any enemy Army movement before it lands: `move_to` resolves the same turn (`resolution.rs:212-250`), and there is no "an Army of the Prospectors stands next door on Attack" line anywhere.
- A Battle as an event: no headline, no Moment unless buildings burn, no marker on the map, no named units, no narrative beyond "3 round(s)".
- The loss of an Army by name; the loss of a Ship by name is one Report line.
- What Hold / Intercept / Evade mean at the point of choosing (the tooltip is on the roster row, not on the stance row).
- Any history: an Army named "the 1st Chinese Army" has no record of where it has fought.

## Proposals (ranked)

1. **Battle Report by name and by beat** — small/medium. `Combatant::name` already exists; pass `ship_name`/`army_name` at `resolution.rs:68, 78` and stop stripping in `run_melee`. Then "TSV Valiant took 2 hits; PMV Aurora escaped". Engine data exists; risk: the simulate log reads the same text (the log lines change too, which the sweep tooling may parse).
2. **Make a Battle a Report line and a real Moment** — medium. Emit a `LineKind::DecisiveBattle` line for every battle ("Battle in Mars orbit: you attacked with 2 Frigates against 3 Factions; nobody lost a hull; Orbital Control unchanged") and fire the Moment when *any unit* is destroyed, including `destroy_army`, with the Army's name and home ("the 1st Chinese Army was destroyed at Russia"). Engine only; rule stays in the engine. Risk: Moment frequency — the sweep says ~55 battles per 80 games, so low.
3. **Odds preview that names the enemy** — small. Button hover (through `cost_button_with_hover`) of six lines: "Russia: neutral, defended by the 1st Russian Army, strength 3, damage 0/5. 61% is the chance to win the first exchange. Moving costs nothing; the Army arrives on Attack." `defenders_at` and `army_name` exist. Same hover for "Land the Army to attack".
4. **Threat line on a held Region** — medium. Beside the Influence challenger line (ticket #262), a military one: "The Prospectors' 2nd European Army (strength 4) stands in the EU, adjacent, on Attack." Data exists (`armies`, `stance`, `neighbours`); a new engine helper `nearest_army_threat(place)`. Risk: it reveals a rival's stance — a disclosure question for the designer.
5. **Move the Army orders block up the card, or into the Armies list** — small. Buttons under each Army in the Armies list (`4609-4618`) rather than after Pioneers and Unrest. Pure UI.
6. **A war line on the map** — medium. Draw the Battle of the last Resolution as a marker on the Body/Region (aggressor colour, one turn), and a shield outline or damage pip on Army shields that fought. `report.battles` carries place and aggressor. Risk: place is a display string, so a `ReportPlace` field is needed on `BattleLine`.
7. **Stance words on the row** — small. Hover on each stance label with the roster's one-liners (`3271-3276`); "Intercept: fights what arrives this turn, before it can land" is the one nobody can discover.
8. **Named Army history** — large. Battles fought, hits landed, places taken, on the roster row hover; new engine state per Army and a save-format change. Flavour-heavy: by the designer's standing rule, likely cut unless the lift shrinks.

## Open questions for the designer
- Should a rival Army's *stance* be visible to the player (proposal 4), or only its presence and strength, as the shields show now?
- Should the odds figure become a whole-battle estimate (a Monte Carlo of `combat::melee`, engine-side) or stay first-round with an honest label?
- Is a Battle with no losses worth a Moment, or only a headline?
- Should the Army orders live on the roster row (click the Army, get its targets) rather than on the Region card at all?
