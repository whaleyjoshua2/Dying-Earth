# The CO2 sweep of version 0.1.0.0 (ticket #506)

Eight cells, each 80 games (twenty seeds by four seatings) on the finished fifty-turn,
twenty-Region board, with only the two climate figures varied; every bar, pace table and card of
the ticket in place. Wins are Custodians / Prospectors / Arkwrights / Archivists. The designer's
ideal is about 15 each and 20 collapses.

**The Sink** is `natural_sink`, the ppm the world takes back each turn. **The step** is `ppm_step`,
the ppm it takes to add half a degree: higher is more forgiving. At 330, the thirty-six-turn
figure, the board would have collapsed almost everywhere; 330 x 50/36 = 458 is what the length
alone would ask for.

**Read.** (The grid ran before the Deep Core Survey card was made askable; the closing sweep at the
cell taken, `ticket-506.txt`, reads 6 / 8 / 35 / 9 and 22.) The dials set the collapses and barely touch the win column: the Arkwrights win 31 to 43
of 80 in every cell. 440 at the Sink of 8 is the nearest to 20 and the nearer of the two 22s to 458.
The raw files are `ticket-506-grid.txt` (400, 460, 520 at Sinks 8 and 10) and `ticket-506-grid-2.txt`
(430, 440 at Sink 8); each prints its cells by seat, and this table sums them by Faction.
