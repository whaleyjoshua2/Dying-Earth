DYING EARTH - playtest build, version 0.1.0.3
=============================================

Thanks for playing. This is an early build of a turn-based strategy game about colonizing
the solar system before global warming wrecks Earth. It is unfinished and unbalanced on
purpose: the point of this playtest is to find out what is confusing, what is boring,
and what breaks.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine). Keep dying-earth.exe and the
   "assets" folder together; the game reads its rules and pictures from there.
2. Double-click dying-earth.exe.
3. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the program is not
   signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The very first launch shows a blank dark window for up to ten seconds while it
   compiles graphics. Later launches are quick.
5. Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and any graphics card from the last ten years.
6. Saves from 0.1.0.2, 0.1.0.1 and 0.1.0.0 load. Saves from 0.09.9 and before do not: the
   Load screen says so.
7. This version ships for Windows and for Linux. On Linux, read RUN-ON-LINUX.txt in the
   folder instead of steps 2 to 5.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 0.1.0.2
--------------------------
The fair-fight version: most of it takes the slack out of the board, the loops that won
alone, the chores that stole turns, and the rules the computer never used.

EARTH
- ONE BANK TO A REGION, and the Prospectors' Investment Bank counts as one.
- A SCRUBBER costs 4 Energy and 1 Ducat a turn, and a Region holds one per 300 million
  people (India 6, China 5, most Regions 1).
- THE WIND FARM: a new Facility anybody with Clean Power may build, 3 Energy a turn for no
  Fuel and no Emissions, at 1 Ducat a turn; a Mars Module too. The computer builds it.
- POWER BURNS FUEL: a Power Plant, a Reactor or a Generator burns 0.8 Fuel a turn, and one
  short of Fuel runs at a quarter. The computer keeps a reserve.
- INFLUENCE costs 4.5 Ducats a point; the Distress Call card gives +2.
- THE PROSPECTORS open with 60 Materials where the other three open with 80. Every Faction's
  card now says what it opens with, in a line above its signature rule.
- THE ARKWRIGHTS and THE ARCHIVISTS open with 10 Ducats; the other two open with none. The
  card's opening line shows it.
- THE ARKWRIGHTS draw +1 Research a turn for each Body off Earth where they hold a Colonist
  (a station over it counts). The Research income lists it as "Bodies settled".
- ORBITAL DATA CENTERS lifts an Observatory's Research x1.25, from x1.5, and once it stands
  every Tech pick is from four, not three, whoever leads it.
 - THE MARKET re-prices every lot: each 20 you buy this turn makes your next a Ducat dearer, each
   20 you sell a Ducat cheaper, 2 either side of the usual price; everyone's trading moves next
   turn's price. Buy 40 Materials and watch the second 20 cost more.
 - The CHEAP ORE OFFER's refusal takes a third off your Materials next turn, where it was half,
   and your lots stay flat while it stands.
- A STRIP PERMIT is once per Region, ever, and the button and the help say so.

SPACE
- LOADING a Ship from a Colony or a station is free of the Ship's turn, as a lift is, so
  a Ship can load and leave in one turn.
- A SHIP BLOCKS A LANDING only when it is on Blockade in low orbit; a passing or holding
  warship does not. A Passage partner never blocks you. Orbital Control still gates the
  Bombard, the Launch and the starving.
- Every computer seat RACES FOR THE MOON from turn one.
- The Custodians' computer uses PRODUCTION MOVED (an idle Factory makes its pair off Earth
  x2.08), and the Custodians starting in East Asia now leave Earth like the rest.

DIPLOMACY
- An INFLUENCE SPEND that would break an Accord is refused unless you say break (the
  button wants a second click).
- A REFUSED OFFER waits three turns. A computer that you refused a Trade will not offer
  the same two goods again until you refuse it a different Trade. You can COUNTER a Trade
  once, with your own amounts: it answers at once. Letting an Accord run out when the
  partner would have renewed counts as a refusal.
- The CHEAP ORE OFFER: refuse it and Materials are half price for you alone next turn.

VICTORY AND THE CLIMATE
- The ARCHIVE wants 250 Research; the UPLOAD is 16 Colonists, four a turn at most, and the
  Archive draws more Energy as it fills. Modules at a place without the people to run them
  go dark.
- The PROSPECTORS' bar is 5,000 Ducats in the Venture Fund; the Fund counts 0.7 of their
  score, and the Arkwrights' Colonial Capital a tenth of theirs. The Fund's growth is
  shown whole on the card.
- The STABILIZATION RUN counts everything the temperature does, cards and permafrost
  included.

THE TEXT AND THE TOOL
- Station orders take the station's NAME or its colony id as well as the orbit number.
- `change` and `cancel-build` take a building's name as well as its index.
- The "calm enough" line is gone from calm Regions. Several refusals and help lines read
  as the rules do.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
--------------------
Write down anything, however small. Especially:
- The moment you were not sure what to do next.
- Anything you clicked that did nothing, or did something you did not expect.
- THE WIND FARM: did you build it, and did the Fuel for power change what you built?
- THE OFFERS: did the computer's offers feel fair? Did you counter a Trade, and what came
  of it?
- THE MOON: who got there first, and did the race matter?
- THE CUSTODIANS: did Stabilization ever feel within reach?
- The turn the game got boring, if it did, and the turn you felt you had lost.
- Crashes: what you were doing, and the "Seed" number shown on the title screen and
  the game-over screen. With the seed the game can be replayed exactly.

Screenshots help. Print Screen, then paste into a message.
