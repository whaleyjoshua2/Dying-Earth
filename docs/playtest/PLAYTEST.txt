DYING EARTH - playtest build, version 0.09.7
============================================

Thanks for playing. This is an early build of a turn-based strategy game about colonizing
the solar system before global warming wrecks Earth. It is unfinished and unbalanced on
purpose: the point of this playtest is to find out what is confusing, what is boring,
and what breaks.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine). Keep dying-earth.exe and the
   "assets" folder together; the game reads its rules and pictures from there.
2. Double-click dying-earth.exe.
3. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the program is not
   signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The very first launch shows a blank dark window for up to ten seconds while it
   compiles graphics. Later launches are quick.
5. Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and any graphics card from the last ten years.
6. Saves from 0.09.6 load in this version and play by its rules. Saves from 0.09.5 and
   before will not load; the Load screen says so.
7. This version ships for Windows and for Linux. On Linux, read RUN-ON-LINUX.txt in the
   folder instead of steps 2 to 5.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 0.09.6
-------------------------
The tightening version: every side has an opening objective, the report is shorter and
in colour, and several figures moved. The computer plays every rule below.

THE RULES THAT MOVED
- AN OPENING OBJECTIVE FOR EACH SIDE, with no deadline and a reward toward winning:
    Custodians - have a Scrubber working: +0.5 ppm on the Natural Sink, for good.
    Prospectors - a working Investment Bank in three different countries: 75 Ducats
      into the Venture Capital Fund.
    Arkwrights - found a Colony on the Moon, ground or orbit: a free Launch Site in a
      country of yours that has none.
    Archivists - two Research Labs or Observatories working, one off Earth: 10 Research
      into the Archive fund.
  Yours is on the new JOURNAL tab of the Victory window. Rivals' are not shown.
- NEUTRAL COUNTRIES BUILD SEA WALLS once Coastal Engineering is researched and the sea is
  close. The wall comes with the country when you take it, and you pay its keep.
- THE ARCHIVE FUND IS LOST if the colony holding the Archive changes hands. Uploads are
  kept.
- PLANETARY STEWARDSHIP adds 1.5 ppm to the Sink, up from 1.0.
- THE CUSTODIANS' RESEARCH DIRECTIVE adds 0.02 ppm to the Sink a point, up from 0.01.
- A STRIP PERMIT leaves +2 Unrest behind, down from +3.

THE COMPUTER
- IT LEAVES RIVALS' WINNING TECHS UNTIL LAST, and holds back half its Research while one
  is being researched.
- IT GOES AFTER THE ARCHIVE once the fund is half full, if it has cause; the Archivists
  defend it.
- THE CUSTODIANS pour Research into the Sink once Planetary Stewardship stands.

THE WINDOW
- THE REPORT IS IN COLOUR AND SHORTER. Each side's name, and each held country's, is in
  its owner's colour. A side's recruiting is one line.
- THE TEMPERATURE IS A RING AROUND END TURN: red from 8 o'clock toward 4 as the world
  warms, a notch at each Break. Hover it for the figures; click it for the Climate Panel.
- A CLOCK at the right of the top bar shows the real time; hover it to see how long you
  have been playing.
- THE TUTORIAL HAS A SEVENTH NOTE, on Blame, Greenwash and Smear.
- THE FINAL REPORT'S TABLE shows what each side made over the whole game, with what it
  had left in brackets.
- SIX HOVERS ARE SHORTER: a country's own Army, war emissions, what a Relay shows, battle
  odds, the Blockade, and Widgets.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
--------------------
Write down anything, however small. Especially:
- The moment you were not sure what to do next.
- Anything you clicked that did nothing, or did something you did not expect.
- THE OPENING OBJECTIVE: did you notice it? Did you go for it? Was the reward worth it?
- THE RING: did you read the temperature off it, or still open the Climate Panel?
- THE REPORT: is it easier to see who did what? Is anything now too short to understand?
- THE ARCHIVE: as the Archivists, did you guard it? As a rival, did you go for it?
- The turn the game got boring, if it did, and the turn you felt you had lost.
- Crashes: what you were doing, and the "Seed" number shown on the title screen and
  the game-over screen. With the seed the game can be replayed exactly.

Screenshots help. Print Screen, then paste into a message.

KNOWN ROUGH EDGES
-----------------
All the numbers below come from eighty computer-only games, twenty with each side going
first. These are known. You do not need to report them.

WHAT THIS VERSION KNOWS ABOUT ITSELF

- THE WIN COLUMN: Custodians 8, Prospectors 21, Arkwrights 18, Archivists 5, where last
  version was 5 / 3 / 11 / 15. The opening objectives moved it most; why they moved it
  so far is not yet known.
- THE WORLD COLLAPSES IN 28 GAMES OF 80, where it was 46.
- THE ARCHIVISTS SCORE NOTHING at the end in 51 games of 80.
- THE COMPUTER ARKWRIGHTS reach the Moon late: they meet their objective in 47 games of
  80, around turns 15 to 29.
- THE ARCHIVE IS TAKEN in about one game in four between computers.
- THE SLIDER FOR THE VENTURE FUND AND THE RESEARCH DIRECTIVE ON THE TOP BAR IS SHORT, so
  fine settings are easier in the Victory window and the Tech Tree.

CARRIED OVER, STILL TRUE

- THE COMPUTER BUILDS FEW REFINERIES OFF EARTH, so the better refining on the small moons is
  mostly yours.
- THE COMPUTER ALMOST NEVER BUILDS A SHIPYARD ON THE MOON, PHOBOS OR DEIMOS.
- TWO STACKS THAT ATTACK IN ONE ORBIT IN ONE TURN FIGHT TWO BATTLES, one per attack.
- NOBODY CAN ACCEPT OR DECLINE AN ACCORD OFFERED TO THEM - INCLUDING YOU. The game still
  answers on your behalf.
- WHERE EACH SIDE STARTS IS NOT RANDOM. The computer sides always take the same countries
  in the same order.
- RENAMING SHIPS, COLONIES AND STATIONS is still cut.
- Antarctica's three colony sites have labels that overlap each other when you are zoomed
  out.
- No sound.
