DYING EARTH - playtest build, version 0.1.0.1
=============================================

Thanks for playing. This is an early build of a turn-based strategy game about colonizing
the solar system before global warming wrecks Earth. It is unfinished and unbalanced on
purpose: the point of this playtest is to find out what is confusing, what is boring,
and what breaks.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine). Keep dying-earth.exe and the
   "assets" folder together; the game reads its rules and pictures from there.
2. Double-click dying-earth.exe.
3. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the program is not
   signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The very first launch shows a blank dark window for up to ten seconds while it
   compiles graphics. Later launches are quick.
5. Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and any graphics card from the last ten years.
6. Saves from 0.1.0.0 load. Saves from 0.09.9 and before do not: the Load screen says so.
7. This version ships for Windows and for Linux. On Linux, read RUN-ON-LINUX.txt in the
   folder instead of steps 2 to 5.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 0.1.0.0
--------------------------
The counterweight version: most of it leans against whoever runs away. The computer plays
every rule below.

THE COMPUTER
- IT RACES FOR THE RESEARCH LEAD once its Victory gate is in reach: more Research Labs and
  Observatories, less Research to the Sink, the harder the nearer it is to winning. As Lead
  it takes its gate as soon as it is offered.
- IT GANGS UP ON A RUNAWAY LEADER, you included: more Influence, Smears, Agitation and
  attacks on the leader, no Accords, Trades or carbon credits for it, and easier Accords
  among the rest.

THE PRICES
- Influence costs 4 Ducats a point. A Strip Permit costs 25 Ducats.
- Every building, Ship and Army costs a tenth more Materials. An Earth Mine makes 4.4;
  every Mine off Earth makes 15% less.

THE RULES
- A SHIP'S TURN is one action. Lifting Pioneers or an Army from Regions, and refuelling,
  stand beside it: lift and leave in the same turn.
- AN ARMY lifts to low orbit with no Launch Site, as Pioneers do.
- ONE LAUNCH SITE to a Region.
- GREENWASH costs Relations, a little with each rival, and it builds up.

WHAT YOU CAN SEE NOW
- The Report says, under its date, your last turn to act on the climate and how far you
  are to your Victory; and the turn before a far world's launch window, that it opens.
- A popup when an Accord of yours ends, and why; a line the turn before it ends.
- A line when a rival spends Influence on a place of yours, and when your home could fall
  to them next turn.
- A Colony Ship shows its people over its room, yellow when it is short of the four that
  found a Colony.
- The Climate Panel's Blame and the country card's emissions and Launch Site moved onto
  mouse-overs.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
--------------------
Write down anything, however small. Especially:
- The moment you were not sure what to do next.
- Anything you clicked that did nothing, or did something you did not expect.
- WHEN YOU LED: did the computer gang up on you? Could you tell? Did it feel fair?
- THE RESEARCH RACE: did a computer seat take the Lead from you to get its gate?
- THE PRICES: did anything feel too dear, or not dear enough?
- A SHIP'S TURN: did lifting and leaving in one turn read clearly?
- The new lines and popups: any you missed, or any that were noise?
- The turn the game got boring, if it did, and the turn you felt you had lost.
- Crashes: what you were doing, and the "Seed" number shown on the title screen and
  the game-over screen. With the seed the game can be replayed exactly.

Screenshots help. Print Screen, then paste into a message.
