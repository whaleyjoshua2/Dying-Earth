DYING EARTH - playtest build, version 0.1.0.4
=============================================

Thanks for playing. This is an early build of a turn-based strategy game about colonizing
the solar system before global warming wrecks Earth. It is unfinished and unbalanced on
purpose: the point of this playtest is to find out what is confusing, what is boring,
and what breaks.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine). Keep dying-earth.exe and the
   "assets" folder together; the game reads its rules and pictures from there.
2. Double-click dying-earth.exe.
3. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the program is not
   signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The very first launch shows a blank dark window for up to ten seconds while it
   compiles graphics. Later launches are quick.
5. Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and any graphics card from the last ten years.
6. Saves from 0.1.0.3, 0.1.0.2, 0.1.0.1 and 0.1.0.0 load. Saves from 0.09.9 and before do
   not: the Load screen says so.
7. This version ships for Windows and for Linux. On Linux, read RUN-ON-LINUX.txt in the
   folder instead of steps 2 to 5.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 0.1.0.3
--------------------------
The storyteller version: most of it makes the game tell its own story in plain words, beside
a few figures that move.

THE STORY
- TURN ONE opens on each Faction's SIGNATURE MOVES, yours first, in place of the Report.
- THE CUSTODIANS get a STABILIZATION METER where the other funds' bars stand: your Emissions,
  the world's, and the Sink.
- THE UPLOAD reads as a story: "4 of Olympus Mons's people were read into the Archive", and
  the card says "10 remembered here".
- THE REPORT: the sea is one line a rise (your losses named, rivals' counted) and each
  building of yours it takes is a line in Your works; a rival taking or occupying your Region
  is the headline; a Region throwing off its holder "revolted"; "Natural Sink +1.5 ppm".
- THE REGION CARD reads "Standing 40 (yours)"; "nobody has any yet" is gone.
- A SHIP'S HOVER reads its name and kind in its Faction's colour: "TSV Beagle [Colony Ship]".
  A Colony's founding date is drawn larger.

THE FIGURES
- THE PROSPECTORS' bar is 5,000 Ducats in the Venture Fund.
- THE ARKWRIGHTS and THE ARCHIVISTS open with 10 Ducats; the other two open with none.
- THE ARKWRIGHTS draw +1 Research a turn for each Body off Earth where they hold a Colonist.
- ORBITAL DATA CENTERS lifts an Observatory's Research x1.25, and once it stands every Tech
  pick is from four, not three.
- THE MARKET re-prices every lot: each 20 you buy this turn makes your next a Ducat dearer,
  each 20 you sell a Ducat cheaper; everyone's trading moves next turn's price.
- The CHEAP ORE OFFER's refusal takes a third off your Materials next turn.
- YOUR VICTORY's gate Tech (and the Archivists' running Archive) is 6% of it, so both bars
  met without the gate reads 94% - needs ..., never 100%.
- CLEAN POWER halves a Power Plant's Emissions (it cut them to 0.4). A MINE emits 0.85 a
  turn, in a Region or in Antarctica, and Clean Manufacturing no longer cuts the Antarctic one.

THE RULES
- PEOPLE IN EARTH ORBIT count for no Victory, and a station there grows a third faster (3% a
  turn). The Archive stands on the ground of another Body, never on a station.
- A NEUTRAL REGION's threshold rises 1 for every Region the taker holds past its first.
- THE COLONIAL CAPITAL needs one open Module slot instead of 24 Colonists, and brings a
  CAPITAL BUILDING you choose, each one only once in the whole game: the Stock Market, Assay
  Office, Fuel Depot, Central Grid or Great Library (+3 of its resource there, +1 at your
  other places on the Body), the Chancery (Influence), the Spaceport (cheaper Ships) or the
  Arcology (faster growth, and calmer Regions at home). Unchosen at End Turn, your Faction's
  own is built. A Blockade stops it at a blockaded place.
- THE COMPUTER races for MARS once the Moon is claimed, and builds up its best place off
  Earth toward a Capital.

THE TEXT AND THE TOOL
- The text tool takes Colonies, Ships and Armies by NAME (`build module tycho habitat`,
  `refuel magellan`, `move-army 1stchinese india`) and shows no numbers for them.
- The help carries no developer history; a few help lines and refusals are clearer.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
--------------------
Write down anything, however small. Especially:
- The moment you were not sure what to do next.
- Anything you clicked that did nothing, or did something you did not expect.
- TURN ONE: did the signature moves tell you what to do first?
- THE CAPITAL: did you reach one, which building did you choose, and why?
- MARS: who got there first, and did the race matter?
- THE CUSTODIANS: did the Stabilization meter make the plan clear?
- THE STORY: which Report lines did you read, and which did you skip?
- The turn the game got boring, if it did, and the turn you felt you had lost.
- Crashes: what you were doing, and the "Seed" number shown on the title screen and
  the game-over screen. With the seed the game can be replayed exactly.

Screenshots help. Print Screen, then paste into a message.
