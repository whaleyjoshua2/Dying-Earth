DYING EARTH - playtest build, version 0.1.0.5
=============================================

Thanks for playing. This is an early build of a turn-based strategy game about colonizing
the solar system before global warming wrecks Earth. It is unfinished and unbalanced on
purpose: the point of this playtest is to find out what is confusing, what is boring,
and what breaks.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine). Keep dying-earth.exe and the
   "assets" folder together; the game reads its rules and pictures from there.
2. Double-click dying-earth.exe.
3. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the program is not
   signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The very first launch shows a blank dark window for up to ten seconds while it
   compiles graphics. Later launches are quick.
5. Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and any graphics card from the last ten years.
6. Saves from 0.1.0.4, 0.1.0.3, 0.1.0.2, 0.1.0.1 and 0.1.0.0 load. Saves from 0.09.9 and
   before do not: the Load screen says so.
7. This version ships for Windows and for Linux. On Linux, read RUN-ON-LINUX.txt in the
   folder instead of steps 2 to 5.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 0.1.0.4
--------------------------
The pushback version: the computer pushes back, and the interface is cut back to its figures.

THE COMPUTER PUSHES BACK
- A COMPUTER FACTION CONTESTS YOUR CLIMB: when your Standing on a neutral Region passes
  half its threshold, the computer seat with the most Standing there, or one holding a
  neighbour, spends to outbid you. It does the same to another computer's climb.
- IT DEFENDS ITS OWN REGIONS: a rival at half the price of a Region it holds, or a quarter
  on its home Region, is answered with Influence; and it keeps its home Region's Standing
  topped up to a floor of 20, so a capital is no longer one turn's spend.
- IT WEIGHS COLLAPSE: a computer seat that reads the world collapsing before its own win
  builds less that emits and more that cleans, until its win is in sight.
- THE PACK forms against a runaway leader at 40% of Victory, where it formed at 50%.

THE FIGURES
- THE PROSPECTORS' bar is 6,250 Ducats in the Venture Fund (5,000 before).
- OFF-WORLD PRESENCE is 20 Colonists for the Custodians and the Prospectors (18); THE
  DIASPORA is 44, still four on each of four Bodies (42). THE ARCHIVE may be ordered only at
  a place of COLONY tier, where it wanted four Colonists living there.
- EVERY FACILITY AND MODULE costs a fifth more Widgets (5, 6, 10 and 14 where 4, 5, 8 and
  12), and a Frigate 6.
- THE SCRUBBER cap is one per 200 million people in a Region (300 before), one to ten.
- THE STRIP PERMIT raises the Region's Baseline Emissions by 0.3 for good (0.2), costs a
  point of Relations with every rival (never an Accord), and the Region does not grow while
  it runs.
- AN OCCUPATION halves the holder's Standing at the place the turn it begins.
- ONE MORE COLONY SLOT on the Moon (Oceanus Procellarum), Phobos (Todd) and Deimos
  (Voltaire).

THE INTERFACE
- TURN ONE opens on your own Faction's SIGNATURE MOVES as one sentence, no longer all four.
- THE REGION CARD's Standing line is one line; what each figure means is on its hover.
- THE TOP BAR's Widgets and Energy figures have hovers that say where each turn's came from,
  place by place.
- THE BATTLE MOMENT says who won: "your TSV Defiant destroyed the Prospectors' PMV Valiant"
  over "you won".
- The CHEAP ORE card's refusal is a button, "Let it go"; a spend that would break an Accord
  reads "breaks Accord"; a Refinery's card says it refuels your Ships in low orbit.
- NINE HOVERS cut to a line each: the Accord's notice, the first landing, Pioneers waiting,
  the Blockade, the Research Directive, the standing order, the Allotment, Unrest and
  Overcrowded.

THE TEXT TOOL
- `show --techs` prints the Tech tree by branch, each Tech's state and effect; the Bodies
  block says who landed first on each world and, until somebody lands, the race to the Moon.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
--------------------
Write down anything, however small. Especially:
- The moment you were not sure what to do next.
- Anything you clicked that did nothing, or did something you did not expect.
- THE CONTEST: did a computer Faction outbid you on a Region you were climbing? Did you
  see it coming, and what did you do about it?
- THE DEFENCE: did you take a computer's home Region, and how many turns did it take?
- THE PROSPECTORS' FUND: at 6,250, did you reach it, and on which turn?
- THE HOVERS: was any one-line hover now too short to tell you the rule?
- The turn the game got boring, if it did, and the turn you felt you had lost.
- Crashes: what you were doing, and the "Seed" number shown on the title screen and
  the game-over screen. With the seed the game can be replayed exactly.

Screenshots help. Print Screen, then paste into a message.
