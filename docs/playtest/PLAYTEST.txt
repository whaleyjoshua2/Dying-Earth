DYING EARTH - playtest build, version 0.1.0.2
=============================================

Thanks for playing. This is an early build of a turn-based strategy game about colonizing
the solar system before global warming wrecks Earth. It is unfinished and unbalanced on
purpose: the point of this playtest is to find out what is confusing, what is boring,
and what breaks.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine). Keep dying-earth.exe and the
   "assets" folder together; the game reads its rules and pictures from there.
2. Double-click dying-earth.exe.
3. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the program is not
   signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The very first launch shows a blank dark window for up to ten seconds while it
   compiles graphics. Later launches are quick.
5. Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and any graphics card from the last ten years.
6. Saves from 0.1.0.1 and 0.1.0.0 load. Saves from 0.09.9 and before do not: the Load
   screen says so.
7. This version ships for Windows and for Linux. On Linux, read RUN-ON-LINUX.txt in the
   folder instead of steps 2 to 5.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 0.1.0.1
--------------------------
The legibility version: nearly all of it is the board and its cards saying what they mean,
plainly, where you can read it.

THE MAPS
- LABELS GIVE WAY instead of piling up, on every map and at every angle. Where two would
  overlap, the less important one shrinks to its name, and hides if even that will not
  fit. The place you have selected and the one under your pointer always show in full;
  then your own places, then rivals', then neutral ones. A shield, a Battle mark or a
  station stays where it is, and a click on a place works whatever its label is doing.

THE CARDS AND MOUSE-OVERS
- Mouse-overs on a line of words (Launch Site: Yes, Influence value, GDP, Widgets) now open
  anywhere on the words. Before, they only opened in the gaps between them.
- A Colony Ship's people count includes the Pioneers you have ordered aboard this turn.
- Fewer words: the top bar's Widgets mouse-over and each card's Widgets line lost their
  explanation of what a Widget is; the Climate Panel no longer lists the card deck.
- Plurals: "1 Facility", "1 turn", "1 Colonist", never "1 Facilities" or "turn(s)".

THE RULES
- NEW YIELDS on the Moon (Mine 1.25, Generator 1.3), Phobos (Generator 1.2, Research 1.2)
  and Deimos (Mine 1.01, Generator 1.1, Refinery 0.52, Research 1.15).
- VICTORY PROGRESS counts both halves of a Victory Condition. "N% of the way there" is now
  a blend of the two parts, not the smaller of them: a full Archive with nobody uploaded
  reads 40%, not 0%. The Archivists' Archive counts 0.4 of it, the Arkwrights' Colonists
  off Earth 0.6, the others half and half. You still need both parts to win. The computer
  judges who is leading by the same figure.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
--------------------
Write down anything, however small. Especially:
- The moment you were not sure what to do next.
- Anything you clicked that did nothing, or did something you did not expect.
- THE LABELS: did a name you needed ever vanish? Did any flicker as you turned the globe?
- THE MOUSE-OVERS: any still hard to open, or any that said too much?
- VICTORY PROGRESS: did the percentage match how close you felt? Did a rival's?
- THE MOONS: did you settle Phobos or Deimos, and was it worth it?
- The turn the game got boring, if it did, and the turn you felt you had lost.
- Crashes: what you were doing, and the "Seed" number shown on the title screen and
  the game-over screen. With the seed the game can be replayed exactly.

Screenshots help. Print Screen, then paste into a message.
