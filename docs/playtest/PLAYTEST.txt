DYING EARTH - playtest build, version 0.1.0.0
=============================================

Thanks for playing. This is an early build of a turn-based strategy game about colonizing
the solar system before global warming wrecks Earth. It is unfinished and unbalanced on
purpose: the point of this playtest is to find out what is confusing, what is boring,
and what breaks.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine). Keep dying-earth.exe and the
   "assets" folder together; the game reads its rules and pictures from there.
2. Double-click dying-earth.exe.
3. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the program is not
   signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The very first launch shows a blank dark window for up to ten seconds while it
   compiles graphics. Later launches are quick.
5. Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and any graphics card from the last ten years.
6. Saves from 0.09.9 and before do not load: the Load screen says so.
7. This version ships for Windows and for Linux. On Linux, read RUN-ON-LINUX.txt in the
   folder instead of steps 2 to 5.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 0.09.9
-------------------------
The more-room version: a bigger solar system, a bigger Earth, a longer game, and a deeper
Tech tree. The computer plays every rule below.

MORE ROOM
- THREE NEW WORLDS: Mercury, Ceres and Vesta, with six Colony slots and two station slots
  each. Ceres and Vesta are small worlds, so a Mass Driver may stand there. Each has its
  own launch windows; Ceres to Vesta needs none.
- TWO NEW COUNTRIES: Vietnam (the South-East Asian mainland) cut out of Indonesia, and
  Argentina (the Spanish-speaking mainland) cut out of Brazil. Twenty in all.
- A FOURTH TIER: the Colonial Capital, 24 people and 70 Materials, one to a world, to
  whoever completes it first, on the ground only. It adds 1 Influence a turn to its
  holder.

A LONGER GAME
- FIFTY TURNS, January 2030 to March 2038. The Victory bars grew with it: the Fund 3,500;
  the Archive 225 Research and 18 uploads; the Diaspora 42 people over four worlds; four
  Stabilization phases in a row; 18 people off Earth for the Custodians and Prospectors.
- THE CLIMATE was refitted to the longer game: it takes 620 ppm to add half a degree now.
- THREE NEW CARDS with a choice: the Carbon Levy, the Deep Core Survey, the Trade Pact.

THE TECH TREE
- THE VICTORY GATES moved to a fourth rung at 62 Research, each behind a 48-Research
  Tech of its own branch: Clean Manufacturing, Regolith Printing (cheaper Modules off
  Earth), Rail Gun Freight (a Mass Driver pays 3 Ducats a turn) and Superconductors
  (Energy x1.25). Every road to a gate is 192 Research.
- PROPULSION runs in two lanes: Orbital Refuelling, Cryogenic Tanks, Nuclear Rockets;
  Clean Propellant, Efficient Transit (now Fuel x0.75), Hardened Hulls. Missile
  Technology needs both ends, on the fourth rung at 58.

THE JOURNAL AND THE FACTIONS
- A SECOND OBJECTIVE for each Faction opens once the first is met: three Leapfrogs, a
  Region at Industry Level 5, a settlement beyond Earth's sphere, three Observatories.
- ACCORDS RUN FIVE TURNS. In the last turn a Renew button on the Accord's line extends it,
  if the other side is willing; Terms can be added to an Accord that stands, and a
  research agreement may be offered. Hover a held Accord to read its Terms.
- THE ARCHIVE draws 20 Energy a turn once complete (12 before); Closed-Loop Colonies takes
  a quarter off a Module's Energy, not half.
- THE ARKWRIGHTS' SHIPS carry half again what everyone else's do, not twice; and to win
  they must hold a Colonial Capital off Earth as well as their Diaspora.

THE MAP
- THE SOLAR SYSTEM MAP IS IN THREE DIMENSIONS: each world's ring lies in its real orbital
  plane. Drag up and down to tilt the view, sideways to turn it.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
--------------------
Write down anything, however small. Especially:
- The moment you were not sure what to do next.
- Anything you clicked that did nothing, or did something you did not expect.
- THE NEW WORLDS: did you go to Mercury, Ceres or Vesta? Was the window line clear?
- THE CAPITAL: did you raise one? Did the 24 people feel reachable?
- FIFTY TURNS: did the game drag in the middle? When did you first feel ahead or behind?
- ACCORDS: did you renew one? Did you notice one lapse?
- THE 3D MAP: did tilting it help you read the launch windows, or get in the way?
- The turn the game got boring, if it did, and the turn you felt you had lost.
- Crashes: what you were doing, and the "Seed" number shown on the title screen and
  the game-over screen. With the seed the game can be replayed exactly.

Screenshots help. Print Screen, then paste into a message.
