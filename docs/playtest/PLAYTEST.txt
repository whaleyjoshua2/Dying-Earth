DYING EARTH - playtest build, version 0.09.5
============================================

Thanks for playing. This is an early build of a turn-based strategy game about colonizing
the solar system before global warming wrecks Earth. It is unfinished and unbalanced on
purpose: the point of this playtest is to find out what is confusing, what is boring,
and what breaks.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine). Keep dying-earth.exe and the
   "assets" folder together; the game reads its rules and pictures from there.
2. Double-click dying-earth.exe.
3. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the program is not
   signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The very first launch shows a blank dark window for up to ten seconds while it
   compiles graphics. Later launches are quick.
5. Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and any graphics card from the last ten years.
6. Saves from 0.09.3 and 0.09.4 load in this version and play by its rules. Saves from
   0.09.2 and before will not load; the Load screen says so.
7. This version ships for Windows and for Linux. On Linux, read RUN-ON-LINUX.txt in the
   folder instead of steps 2 to 5.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 0.09.4
-------------------------
The fog version: you no longer see everything your rivals do. The Tech tree is redrawn so
each side's winning Tech ends a line of its own. The computer plays every rule below.

THE RULES THAT MOVED
- FOG OF WAR. Off Earth you see a world where you have a colony, a station, a ship or a
  working Relay. On Earth you see the countries you hold and their neighbours; a working
  Embassy shows all of Earth. Out of sight, rival ships and armies are only counted, and
  their builds, earnings and report lines are hidden.
- GOOD RELATIONS LIFT THE FOG. A rival Cordial to you shows its stockpiles and income; a
  Friendly one shows what it is doing. An Accord shows both. The computer sees only what
  you would in its place.
- THE TECH TREE IS REDRAWN. Each side's winning Tech ends a line: Prospectors Extraction,
  Arkwrights Off-world Living, Archivists Society, Custodians Stewardship (Industry
  renamed). Public Science is now LARGE LANGUAGE MODELS.
- FOUR PREREQUISITES MOVED: Missile Technology needs Nuclear Rockets, not Hardened Hulls;
  Automated Refining needs only Deep Mining; Closed-Loop Colonies needs only Expanded
  Habitats; Efficient Transit needs Orbital Refuelling.
- COMMODITY FINANCE, a new Extraction Tech (32), makes Banks, Investment Banks, Trade Posts
  and Exchanges pay 15% more. The Extraction Charter needs it instead of Beneficiation.
- ORBITAL DATA CENTERS, a new Society Tech (32), makes Observatories half again. The Upload
  needs it.
- PIONEERS: up to two a turn from EACH country you hold (Arkwrights four), where it was
  four a turn from one country. Each one calms its country by an eighth of a point.

THE WINDOW
- EVERY PIONEER ORDER IS A SLIDER: recruit, the sea, the lift and loading a colony ship.
- MOVING AN EMPTY COLONY SHIP OR CARRIER WARNS YOU: "Empty." on its card, and a right-click
  move asks before it goes.
- THE REPORT IS SHORTER. Your own news comes first; the other headings are folded shut
  with a count. Click one to open it.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
--------------------
Write down anything, however small. Especially:
- The moment you were not sure what to do next.
- Anything you clicked that did nothing, or did something you did not expect.
- THE FOG: did you miss knowing what a rival was up to? Did you build an Embassy or a Relay
  to see, or court a rival's friendship for it?
- THE REPORT: did you open the folded headings, or was your own news enough?
- THE TREE: could you see your own road to victory at a glance?
- PIONEERS: was the slider quicker than the old buttons?
- The turn the game got boring, if it did, and the turn you felt you had lost.
- Crashes: what you were doing, and the "Seed" number shown on the title screen and
  the game-over screen. With the seed the game can be replayed exactly.

Screenshots help. Print Screen, then paste into a message.

KNOWN ROUGH EDGES
-----------------
All the numbers below come from eighty computer-only games, twenty with each side going
first. These are known. You do not need to report them.

WHAT THIS VERSION KNOWS ABOUT ITSELF

- THE WIN COLUMN: Custodians 10, Prospectors 10, Arkwrights 1, Archivists 17, where last
  version was 8 / 7 / 2 / 17.
- THE WORLD COLLAPSES IN 42 GAMES OF 80, where it was 46.
- THE WHOLE TECH TREE FINISHES IN 65 GAMES OF 80, around turn 25, where it was 73.
- THE ARKWRIGHTS SCORE NOTHING at the end in 58 games of 80.
- THE FOG DOES NOT HURT THE COMPUTER: with the fog lifted the same eighty games end the
  same way.

CARRIED OVER, STILL TRUE

- THE COMPUTER BUILDS FEW REFINERIES OFF EARTH, so the better refining on the small moons is
  mostly yours.
- THE COMPUTER ALMOST NEVER BUILDS A SHIPYARD ON THE MOON, PHOBOS OR DEIMOS.
- TWO STACKS THAT ATTACK IN ONE ORBIT IN ONE TURN FIGHT TWO BATTLES, one per attack.
- NOBODY CAN ACCEPT OR DECLINE AN ACCORD OFFERED TO THEM - INCLUDING YOU. The game still
  answers on your behalf.
- WHERE EACH SIDE STARTS IS NOT RANDOM. The computer sides always take the same countries
  in the same order.
- RENAMING SHIPS, COLONIES AND STATIONS is still cut.
- Antarctica's three colony sites have labels that overlap each other when you are zoomed
  out.
- No sound.
