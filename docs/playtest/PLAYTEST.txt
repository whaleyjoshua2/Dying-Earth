DYING EARTH - playtest build, version 0.09.4
============================================

Thanks for playing. This is an early build of a turn-based strategy game about colonizing
the solar system before global warming wrecks Earth. It is unfinished and unbalanced on
purpose: the point of this playtest is to find out what is confusing, what is boring,
and what breaks.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine). Keep dying-earth.exe and the
   "assets" folder together; the game reads its rules and pictures from there.
2. Double-click dying-earth.exe.
3. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the program is not
   signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The very first launch shows a blank dark window for up to ten seconds while it
   compiles graphics. Later launches are quick.
5. Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and any graphics card from the last ten years.
6. Saves from 0.09.3 load in this version and play by its rules. Saves from 0.09.2 and
   before will not load; the Load screen says so.
7. This version ships for Windows and for Linux. On Linux, read RUN-ON-LINUX.txt in the
   folder instead of steps 2 to 5.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 0.09.3
-------------------------
The Custodians' version: their climate game counts now. Research comes from every country,
and fuel is cheaper to make and buy. The computer plays every rule below.

THE RULES THAT MOVED
- RESEARCH COMES FROM EVERY COUNTRY, by its people and its schooling. A Research Lab makes
  that country's research half again (x1.5). Countries nobody holds add half theirs to the
  shared Tech. The whole tree now finishes in most games.
- THE FIRST TURN THE WORLD IS UNDER THE NATURAL SINK calms every country by half a point,
  a whole point where the Custodians hold it. Once a game.
- THE CUSTODIANS' VICTORY LINE says how far over the Sink the world is, in ppm, and a run
  under it earns part credit before it reaches three turns.
- A NATURE RESERVE, a new building for everyone (30 materials, 5 widgets), adds 1 to the
  Natural Sink and softens climate unrest in its country.
- THE ARCHIVISTS, leading a Tech to completion, win 5 Influence and half a point of Unrest
  off every country they hold.
- THE ARKWRIGHTS calm every country they hold by a point for each colony they found off
  Earth, and half a point for each station.
- ORBITAL REFUELLING, a new rung-1 Tech, cuts every crossing by a tenth.
- COLONY SHIPS HOLD 40 FUEL; warships hold 30. CRYOGENIC TANKS, a new rung-2 Tech, adds
  15 to every tank. A ship is built full, so the fuel is paid at the yard.
- FUEL IS CHEAPER: a refinery makes 4 on Earth and 4.5 off it, the Moon, Phobos and Deimos
  refine a quarter better, and the market sells fuel at 3 ducats.
- MOTHBALLING says its price: +1 Unrest in that country, +2 to decommission.

THE WINDOW
- A COLONY SHIP UNLOADS ANY NUMBER of colonists, with a slider, and founds with any number.
- HOVER A TECH and the tree lights the path to it. The boxes are a tenth smaller.
- EACH SIDE'S VICTORY BARS are in its own colour.
- HOVER A FIGURE ON A PLACE'S OUTPUT ROW to see what made it.
- FEWER WORDS in the report and on the cards.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
--------------------
Write down anything, however small. Especially:
- The moment you were not sure what to do next.
- Anything you clicked that did nothing, or did something you did not expect.
- THE CUSTODIANS: could you tell how close you were to winning? Did a Nature Reserve feel
  worth a slot?
- RESEARCH: did it matter which countries you held, and did a School feel worth it?
- FUEL: with the bigger colony ship, did you run short?
- The turn the game got boring, if it did, and the turn you felt you had lost.
- Crashes: what you were doing, and the "Seed" number shown on the title screen and
  the game-over screen. With the seed the game can be replayed exactly.

Screenshots help. Print Screen, then paste into a message.

KNOWN ROUGH EDGES
-----------------
All the numbers below come from eighty computer-only games, twenty with each side going
first. These are known. You do not need to report them.

WHAT THIS VERSION KNOWS ABOUT ITSELF

- THE WIN COLUMN: Custodians 8, Prospectors 7, Arkwrights 2, Archivists 17, where last
  version was 7 / 5 / 1 / 7. The Archivists gained most, with research.
- THE WORLD COLLAPSES IN 46 GAMES OF 80, where it was 60.
- THE WHOLE TECH TREE FINISHES IN 73 GAMES OF 80, around turn 24.
- THE ARKWRIGHTS SCORE NOTHING at the end in 57 games of 80.
- THE COMPUTER BUILDS FEW REFINERIES OFF EARTH, so the better refining on the small moons is
  mostly yours.

CARRIED OVER, STILL TRUE

- THE COMPUTER ALMOST NEVER BUILDS A SHIPYARD ON THE MOON, PHOBOS OR DEIMOS.
- TWO STACKS THAT ATTACK IN ONE ORBIT IN ONE TURN FIGHT TWO BATTLES, one per attack.
- NOBODY CAN ACCEPT OR DECLINE AN ACCORD OFFERED TO THEM - INCLUDING YOU. The game still
  answers on your behalf.
- WHERE EACH SIDE STARTS IS NOT RANDOM. The computer sides always take the same countries
  in the same order.
- RENAMING SHIPS, COLONIES AND STATIONS is still cut.
- Antarctica's three colony sites have labels that overlap each other when you are zoomed
  out.
- No sound.
