DYING EARTH - playtest build, version 0.09.6
============================================

Thanks for playing. This is an early build of a turn-based strategy game about colonizing
the solar system before global warming wrecks Earth. It is unfinished and unbalanced on
purpose: the point of this playtest is to find out what is confusing, what is boring,
and what breaks.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine). Keep dying-earth.exe and the
   "assets" folder together; the game reads its rules and pictures from there.
2. Double-click dying-earth.exe.
3. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the program is not
   signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The very first launch shows a blank dark window for up to ten seconds while it
   compiles graphics. Later launches are quick.
5. Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and any graphics card from the last ten years.
6. SAVES FROM 0.09.5 AND BEFORE WILL NOT LOAD: the map has two new countries. The Load
   screen says so.
7. This version ships for Windows and for Linux. On Linux, read RUN-ON-LINUX.txt in the
   folder instead of steps 2 to 5.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 0.09.5
-------------------------
The growth version: colonies grow on their own, there are more ways to move people and
found places, and the map has sixteen countries. The computer plays every rule below.

THE RULES THAT MOVED
- TWO NEW COUNTRIES. PAKISTAN (with Afghanistan and Central Asia) is cut out of India and
  China; THE UNITED KINGDOM (with Ireland and Iceland) out of the European Union. India no
  longer borders Iran.
- COLONIES GROW: 2% of their Colonists a turn, up to their Habitats' room. A colony under
  Blockade, or with its Core dark, loses one a turn.
- A COLONY SHIP LOADS PIONEERS FROM SEVERAL COUNTRIES in one turn.
- A COLONY SHIP UNLOADS ONTO A STATION of yours, from the ring it sits in.
- FOUND WITHOUT A COLONY SHIP: a ground colony builds a station over its world, and a
  station builds a ground colony below it and sends Colonists down. Otherwise a Colony
  Ship founds as before.
- VENUS HAS NO LOW ORBIT any more. Ships there sit at a station ring.
- COLONY SHIPS HOLD 35 FUEL, down from 40. A ship is built with the Fuel you paid for,
  and cancelling the build gives it back.
- GENERATION SHIPS needs Relay Networks as well as Closed-Loop Colonies.
- THE ARCHIVE costs 150 Research to fund.
- THE CUSTODIANS' INFLUENCE BONUS is 15%, down from 20%.
- A CAPTURED SCRUBBER RUNS AT HALF instead of being destroyed; a quarter for a neutral
  country that takes one back.

THE COMPUTER
- IT USES THE MARKET: buys Energy when short, sells spare Materials and Fuel.
- IT BREAKS BLOCKADES: a Battery first, a standing fleet, and a second shipyard over Earth.
- THE CUSTODIANS PICK ON THE BIGGEST POLLUTER.
- A computer Shipyard builds one Factory, not one every turn.

THE WINDOW
- THE COLONISTS LINE reads 8/12 with a people glyph: amber from three quarters full, red
  when full.
- AN OFFLINE BUILDING SAYS WHY on hover: short of Energy, the card that struck it, the
  grid, occupation, or upkeep unpaid. Blockaded and at-half are said too.
- FIXED: the crash on selecting a Colony Ship at a Venus station.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
--------------------
Write down anything, however small. Especially:
- The moment you were not sure what to do next.
- Anything you clicked that did nothing, or did something you did not expect.
- GROWTH: did you notice colonies growing? Did you build Habitats ahead of it?
- FOUNDING: did you found a station from the ground, or a colony from a station? Was it
  clear you could?
- THE NEW COUNTRIES: did you take Pakistan or the United Kingdom? Were they worth it?
- OFFLINE BUILDINGS: when one went dark, did the hover tell you what to fix?
- THE COMPUTER: did a rival blockade you, or break yours?
- The turn the game got boring, if it did, and the turn you felt you had lost.
- Crashes: what you were doing, and the "Seed" number shown on the title screen and
  the game-over screen. With the seed the game can be replayed exactly.

Screenshots help. Print Screen, then paste into a message.

KNOWN ROUGH EDGES
-----------------
All the numbers below come from eighty computer-only games, twenty with each side going
first. These are known. You do not need to report them.

WHAT THIS VERSION KNOWS ABOUT ITSELF

- THE WIN COLUMN: Custodians 5, Prospectors 3, Arkwrights 11, Archivists 15, where last
  version was 10 / 10 / 1 / 17. Before the two new countries went in, this version read
  15 / 10 / 10 / 5; why they moved it so far is not yet known.
- THE WORLD COLLAPSES IN 46 GAMES OF 80, where it was 42.
- THE WHOLE TECH TREE FINISHES IN 55 GAMES OF 80, around turn 24, where it was 65.
- BLOCKADES ARE RARE between computers: one stood in 4 games of 80.
- COLONIES GROW SLOWLY in computer hands: 379 Colonists over the eighty games.

CARRIED OVER, STILL TRUE

- THE COMPUTER BUILDS FEW REFINERIES OFF EARTH, so the better refining on the small moons is
  mostly yours.
- THE COMPUTER ALMOST NEVER BUILDS A SHIPYARD ON THE MOON, PHOBOS OR DEIMOS.
- TWO STACKS THAT ATTACK IN ONE ORBIT IN ONE TURN FIGHT TWO BATTLES, one per attack.
- NOBODY CAN ACCEPT OR DECLINE AN ACCORD OFFERED TO THEM - INCLUDING YOU. The game still
  answers on your behalf.
- WHERE EACH SIDE STARTS IS NOT RANDOM. The computer sides always take the same countries
  in the same order.
- RENAMING SHIPS, COLONIES AND STATIONS is still cut.
- Antarctica's three colony sites have labels that overlap each other when you are zoomed
  out.
- No sound.
